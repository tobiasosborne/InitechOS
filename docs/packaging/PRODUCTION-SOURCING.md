## 0. Read this first: what is a real price and what is not

Most German online printers price through JavaScript configurators that I could not drive
from this environment, and the binder and box makers that matter quote by e-mail. So:

- **REAL** = a price printed on the supplier's page that I read on 2026-10-04 (with the exact
  configuration it applies to). It is usually not the configuration we need.
- **NO PRICE** = the supplier exists and does the job; a quote or configurator run is needed.
- **ESTIMATE** = my extrapolation from REAL prices, labelled as such. Treat as +/-50%.

Prices include German VAT where the supplier shows gross prices. US prices exclude import
VAT (19%) and customs handling.

**Important cost finding:** in digital short-run printing a book with *any* colour on interior
pages is usually charged at the colour rate for the colour pages or the whole block (the
epubli example prices colour pages separately). A two-colour interior (Ashton-Tate,
WordPerfect style) therefore costs several times a black-only interior. Black-only interiors
(IBM, Borland, Lotus style) are both authentic and cheap. In ring binders the problem
disappears: colour sheets (dividers, covers) are printed separately and collated.

**Mechanisms (parts):** US half-size three-ring mechanisms with 2.75 in (70 mm) spacing and
210 mm length are sold separately: Shuokecrafts "Ring Binder Mechanism, 3 Round Ring, A5 Half
Letter" (Amazon, 20 mm rings; price not captured), Binding101 "half-size 3-ring binder
mechanisms, box of 50", TALAS (US bookbinding supplier, ring book mechanisms). A 210 mm
mechanism fits a 229 mm (9 in) page height.

**Finding:** no EU supplier found offers a US three-ring mechanism off the shelf. The EU
standard is 2-ring or 4-ring (DIN). Options: (a) EU binder maker fits imported mechanisms;
(b) bookbinder builds binders (1-10 copies); (c) US maker at 25-50 minimum (50-copy run only);
(d) accept DIN 4-ring (cheap, but wrong for a US product). Decision D-5 in the main report.

(SRA3 or B2) at a print shop that has a digital cutting/creasing table (plotter cut), fold and
glue by hand; or have a bookbinder make rigid slipcases and print only the **paper sleeve**

printed box for the cost of one large-format print.

**ESTIMATE per box:** 1 copy 20-60 EUR (custom rigid, bookbinder or prototype pricing);
10 copies 10-25 EUR each; 50 copies 4-10 EUR each (madika-class pricing at custom size).

**Can real media still be sourced?** Yes, in 2026 **[READ: suppliers above]**: 5.25-in
1.2 MB and 360 K disks are sold new or new-old-stock in small packs at roughly 1.50-2.50 USD
per disk (FloppyDisk.com) or as NOS in Germany and the Netherlands; 3.5-in HD disks are easier
still. Supply of 5.25-in media is finite and brand/colour mixes vary between packs, so buy the
whole run's media in one lot and test-write every disk. 5.25-in DD (360 K) is scarcer than HD.

**Media fit:** the current images are 1.44 MB (3.5-in HD geometry: `build/flair_data.img` is
1,474,560 bytes). A 5.25-in edition needs 1.2 MB (or 360 K) distribution images and an
installer that spans diskettes; that is software work (main report decision D-7).

## 8. Cards, envelopes, templates

Standard digital print products (postcards, folded cards, envelopes with print) at any
online printer (WIRmachenDRUCK, Flyeralarm, onlineprinters, print24): NO PRICE checked;
these are low-cost commodity items (typically single-digit EUR per 10-50 pieces) **[MEM]**.
Keyboard templates need die-cutting: a print shop with a digital cutting table cuts any
outline from a vector file without a die.

mechanism before committing to binders for more than the pilot product.

## 10. File requirements (common to the printers above)

PDF with all fonts embedded; 3 mm bleed for German printers (print24 Schuber asks 2 mm);
images 300 ppi at final size; CMYK for colour work (FOGRA39/FOGRA51 profiles are the usual
German defaults **[MEM]**); PDF/X-1a or PDF/X-4 where the printer asks for it; spine width
from the printer's calculator for the chosen paper; die-lines as a separate named spot
colour layer for boxes, slipcases and templates.
