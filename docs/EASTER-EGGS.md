# Operator-only Easter Eggs

This file is not shipped and is not a manual or command inventory.

## Jump to Conclusions (FLAIR, initech-2ter part a)

Hold Option (PC Alt) and click the top Apple icon three times, then release
Option. Releasing Option early or clicking elsewhere resets the count. There
is no time limit. No menu item advertises the mat; the About resources are
unchanged. This is an authored hidden gesture: no Mac OS 8 hidden-About
precedent was found in the local system7-decomp corpus.

The modeless Platinum document has ten pads, Chicago 12 labels, and no grow
box. Click a pad to jump the cursor hotspot to its centre and display its
conclusion in a one-button Platinum alert. Return or OK dismisses the alert.
The mat can be moved, covered, uncovered, closed, and discovered again.

The authored contract is `spec/easter/jump_to_conclusions.json`. Conclusions
retain their authored capitalisation:

- It's a Bad Idea.
- Ask Bob.
- PC LOAD LETTER
- Re-Do the Cover Sheet
- Nobody Will Notice
- Take it to Lumbergh
- Move Your Desk
- Just a Moment.
- That Would Be Great.
- Is This Good for the Company?

The DOS verb and SAMIR option belong to later beads.
