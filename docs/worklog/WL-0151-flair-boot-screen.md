# WL-0151 -- The FLAIR boot screen (initech-tdnl.105)

Lane: lane/bootscreen (Claude Opus, taking over a stopped agent; mint authority
in ../system7-decomp). Operator directive 2026-10-17: "flair is missing a boot
screen which must canonically feature the initech logo".

## Context

The bead asks for the Mac OS 8 startup splash -- the centred panel, its logo
cell, its title -- reproduced cell for cell and graded against a real capture,
with the Initech three-cube logo + INITECH wordmark as the mark. No startup
capture existed in the corpus, so this lane minted one first.

## Ground truth

- **mint-009** (`../system7-decomp/re/mint-results-009.md`, committed there as
  `ed8a69d` on master, not pushed): Basilisk II, Quadra 650 ROM, Mac OS 8.1,
  640x480x8, the whole boot grabbed as a frame sequence twice
  (`mint/s8_startup_capture.sh`; the previous agent's runs, reused -- not
  re-minted). Captures (gitignored, regenerable): `s8_startup_gray.png`,
  `s8_startup_welcome.png`, `s8_startup_panel.png`, `s8_startup_strip3.png`,
  `s8_startup_strip7.png`, `s8_startup_last.png`, `s8_startup_after.png`. The
  welcome panel, the empty "Starting Up..." panel, the last panel frame and the
  post-panel frame are pixel-identical across both runs.
- What 8.1 actually shows: 50 % gray (black where x+y is even) with rounded
  black screen corners (widths 5,3,2,1,1); the panel (frame x 109..530 y
  47..369, white top/left highlight, #AAAAAA bottom/right shadow, #EEEEEE bevel
  corners, #DDDDDD face), the inset white well (x 144..494 y 73..296), the logo
  cell (x 219..416 y 92..272) and "Welcome to Mac OS" in **Charcoal** 12 with
  caps on rows 317..325. Then the CLUT switches to Platinum (same pixels map
  DD->E7, AA->C0, EE->F3), the desktop picture comes up behind the panel, the
  title becomes "Starting Up..." with a progress bar (x 240..399 y 338..349) and
  the extension icons fill a strip along the bottom (cells x = 8 + 40k).
- The logo: `pubs/common/initech-logo.tex` and DESIGN-REFERENCE Sec 7.0/7.1.

## What changed

- `spec/assets/boot_logo.h` -- the lockup as an 8-bit-index strike, u = 16 px,
  245 x 168, **1856 RLE bytes** (one byte per run: role << 6 | len-1), four
  ROLES (clear/top/dark/light), generated by `tools/boot_logo_gen.c` (scanline
  fill over the TikZ polygons, the C's arcs tessellated). Header says AUTHORED
  from the measured geometry. The mark's centre line is snapped to a pixel
  boundary, so every vertical block edge falls between pixel columns.
- `os/milton/boot_screen.h` -- the pure painter: 640x480 indices row by row
  into a sink. Furniture from the capture coordinates; the strike centred on
  the measured logo cell (318, 182); "Welcome to InitechOS." in Chicago 12
  (FLAIR's system font -- a documented deviation from Charcoal), centred on the
  panel, caps on the capture's rows. Colours (policy): panel greys DD/AA/EE
  from the gray ramp; cube tops CIDX_DESKTOP #8DDCDC (initech-9222 pending);
  dark faces + logotype CIDX_BLACK (Initech Black is 0/0/0/100 in Sec 7.1);
  light faces gray 212 (the sampled 213/212/212).
- `os/milton/kmain.c` -- `flair_boot_screen()` after FLAIR-HEAP-OK in every
  FLAIR kernel (BOOT_FLAIR_SHELL and BOOT_FLAIR_LIVE): loads the FLAIR DAC at
  8bpp, writes indices (8bpp) or `flair_palette_rgb` (24/32bpp) through
  `surface_put_pixel`, prints `FLAIR-BOOT-SCREEN`. No splash on a 320x200
  machine. `flair_boot_screen_hold()` polls PIT channel 2 (mode 0, OUT2 on port
  0x61 bit 5; IRQs are still masked) for `FLAIR_BOOT_HOLD_TICKS` right before
  the first desktop present: 200 (~2 s) under `-DFLAIR_LIVE_INTERACTIVE`, 0 --
  compiled out -- everywhere else.
- `os/milton/int21.c/.h`, `kmain.c flair_launch_text_tenant` -- the SAMIR
  hotkey fix (Frictions).
- Makefile: the gates below; `boothold` / `bootnologo` kmain variants (the
  template, one knob); the 8bpp VESA 0x101 copy of the boot-hold image + its
  fixture contract; the `boot_screen` clip block (before record-flair) and a
  `RECORD_KEYS_AFTER_<script>` override (default FLAIR-LIVE-READY); every
  `kmain*.o` rebuilds on the boot-screen headers (.EXTRA_PREREQS).
- Merged origin/main into the lane once (current VESA fixture contracts; two
  additive Makefile conflict blocks, both kept).

## Frictions

- **Canon slots moved under the lane.** Main's Finder icon lane made indices
  11..25 named colours; idx 19 (my first choice for Initech Black) became
  #E70000 and the merged gate drew a red logo -- caught by LOGO. The dark role
  is now CIDX_BLACK; greys come only from the ramp above the named block.
- **A second copy of Chicago.** Reading `chicago12_rows` directly linked a
  3,136-byte table the kernel otherwise stores packed; the title now goes
  through `text.h chicago_cell_bits`.
- **SAMIR hotkey race (pre-existing, now fixed).** `test-samir-lifetime-desktop`
  was flaky on main (1 of 5 runs RED: `. \use clients.dbf`) and RED every run
  once the 2 s hold made the harness inject right at FLAIR-LIVE-READY. The
  launch drained g_kbd only: the '\' can already sit in the INT 21h lookahead
  (AH=0Bh / the ^C check of a CON output byte), and the next keys can already be
  queued (a full drain then ate SAMIR's 'u'). `int21_conin_discard_through('\\')`
  drops pending input through the hotkey, lookahead first, and keeps the
  typeahead; host oracle in test_conin + INT21_MUTATE_DISCARD_RING_ONLY RED;
  4/4 green with the hold.
- The interactive images reach FLAIR-LIVE-READY ~2 s later, so `appl-boot`,
  `record-flair` and the SAMIR desktop driver now pass `--keys-after-ms 15000`
  (the wait still ends at the marker).
- A known red at the base: the lane branch predated main's VESA 0x101 contract
  fix (`IO.SYS=SHR`); merging main resolved it.
