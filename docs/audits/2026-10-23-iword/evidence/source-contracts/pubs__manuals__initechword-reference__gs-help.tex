9: \WPHead{Getting Help}
10: InitechWord has no help screens: \K{F3}, the Help key of WordPerfect, shows
11: the word \textit{Help} in grey on the status line and nothing more.  Help
12: with InitechWord is in its publications and at the Initech Help Desk.

26: \WPSide{This Reference}
27: This binder is the reference for every feature of InitechWord.  It has four
28: sections, each behind a divider:
29: \begin{description}
30: \item[Getting Started] what you need to know before you begin, and a short
31: lesson.
32: \item[Reference] one entry for every feature, in alphabetical order.  An
33: entry begins with what the feature does, then gives the steps to use it.
34: Under \textit{Notes} it says how the feature works with others, and
35: \textit{See Also} names the entries that tell you more.
36: \item[Appendix] lists to look things up in: the characters, the codes, the
37: messages, the document file, the settings InitechWord starts with, the
38: keyboard, the menus and the limits.
39: \item[Glossary/Index] the words this book uses, and the index.
40: \end{description}
41: Entries are named the way you would look for them, the general word first:
42: the entry for a hard return is \textit{Return, Hard}, beside \textit{Return,
43: Soft}.  When one entry sends you to another it names the book as well, as in
44: ``see \InRef{Block}.''

46: \WPSide{The Workbook}
47: The \textit{InitechWord Workbook} teaches InitechWord in lessons.  Each
48: lesson produces a real office document, and you type each keystroke as the
49: lesson gives it.  If you have not used a word processor before, work through
50: the lessons of the first section of the Workbook in order before you use
51: this Reference.
