25: \WPAppendix{A}{Characters}
26: InitechWord takes the characters of the keyboard that print: the space and
27: the 94 characters from \Scr{!} to \Scr{\textasciitilde}.  Each is stored in
28: the document as the one byte of its ASCII code, as WordPerfect 5.1 stores
29: it.  The table gives each character with its decimal code.
30: 
31: \par\addvspace{6pt}
32: \directlua{
33:   local out = {}
34:   for c = 32, 126 do
35:     local ch = string.char(c)
36:     local shown
37:     if c == 32 then shown = "sp"
38:     elseif ch:match("[A-Za-z0-9]") then shown = ch
39:     else shown = "\string\\char" .. c .. "\string\\relax " end
40:     out[\string#out + 1] = c .. " & \string\\texttt{" .. shown .. "}"
41:   end
42:   local rows = {}
43:   local per = 19
44:   for r = 1, per do
45:     local cells = {}
46:     for col = 0, 4 do
47:       local i = r + col * per
48:       if out[i] then cells[\string#cells + 1] = out[i] else cells[\string#cells + 1] = " & " end
49:     end
50:     rows[\string#rows + 1] = table.concat(cells, " & ") .. "\string\\\string\\"
51:   end
52:   tex.sprint("\string\\noindent{\string\\small\string\\begin{tabular}{@{}rl@{\string\\quad}rl@{\string\\quad}rl@{\string\\quad}rl@{\string\\quad}rl@{}}" .. table.concat(rows) .. "\string\\end{tabular}}")
53: }
54: \par\addvspace{6pt}
55: 
56: The keys that are not characters -- the function keys, Enter, Tab and the
57: others -- do not put characters in the document; they put codes there or
58: move the cursor (see Appendix F).

63: as \Code{[ ]}: a space at which a line never wraps.  InitechWord keeps hard
64: spaces in documents it retrieves; it has no key that types one.

128: \Notes
129: \Topic{Codes Reveal Codes Does Not Show}
130: WordPerfect 5.1 keeps some records in a document that Reveal Codes never
131: shows: one at the end of an indented paragraph, one before each hard page,
132: one at the start of each centered or right-justified line, and one at the
133: end of a centered or flush-right line.  InitechWord writes them as
134: WordPerfect does and keeps them up to date as you edit; you never see or
135: delete them.

226: \WPAppendix{D}{Document File Format}
227: An InitechWord document is a WordPerfect 5.1 document file: InitechWord
228: writes the file as WordPerfect 5.1 writes it, and reads the documents
229: WordPerfect 5.1 saves, as long as they hold only the codes listed in
230: Appendix B.  This appendix describes the file for programmers.  All numbers
231: of more than one byte are stored with the low byte first.
232: 
233: \WPSide{The Header}
234: The first 16 bytes of the file are its header:
235: \par\addvspace{4pt}
236: \noindent\begin{tabular}{@{}llp{70mm}@{}}
237: \textbf{Offset} & \textbf{Bytes} & \textbf{Contents}\\
238: 0 & 4 & the byte FFh and the letters \Scr{WPC}\\
239: 4 & 4 & where the text of the document begins (the offset of the body)\\
240: 8 & 1 & 1, the product\\
241: 9 & 1 & 0Ah, a document\\
242: 10 & 1 & 0, the major version\\
243: 11 & 1 & 1, the minor version\\
244: 12 & 2 & 0: the document is not locked\\
245: 14 & 2 & 0\\
246: \end{tabular}
247: \par\addvspace{4pt}
248: 
249: \WPSide{The Prefix}
250: Between the header and the body are the settings of the document, in
251: packets.  A new InitechWord document is saved with the 327 bytes WordPerfect
252: 5.1 writes for a new document (the body begins at offset 327, 147h); one
253: byte of them records whether the cursor was at the end of the document when
254: it was saved.  A document that was retrieved is saved with the prefix it
255: came with.
256: 
257: \WPSide{The Body}
258: The body is the text and its codes, byte by byte:
259: \par\addvspace{4pt}
260: \noindent\begin{tabular}{@{}lp{76mm}@{}}
261: \textbf{Bytes} & \textbf{Meaning}\\
262: 20h--7Fh & a character (Appendix A)\\
263: 0Ah & hard return \Code{[HRt]}\\
264: 0Ch & hard page \Code{[HPg]}\\
265: 0Dh & soft return \Code{[SRt]}\\
266: 83h & end of a centered or flush-right line\\
267: 90h & deletable soft return \Code{[DSRt]}\\
268: A0h & hard space \Code{[ ]}\\
269: C0h--CFh & a fixed-length code that begins and ends with the same byte\\
270: D0h--FFh & a variable-length code: the byte, a sub-code, a two-byte length,
271: the contents, the length, the sub-code and the byte again\\
272: \end{tabular}
273: \par\addvspace{4pt}
274: 
275: \WPSide{Fixed-Length Codes}
276: \begin{tabular}{@{}llp{64mm}@{}}
277: \textbf{Code} & \textbf{Length} & \textbf{Meaning}\\
278: C1h & 9 & center, flush right or tab; the sub-code 02h is \Code{[Tab]}\\
279: C2h & 11 & \Code{[\rarr Indent]}\\
280: C3h & 3 & an attribute on: C3h, the attribute number, C3h\\
281: C4h & 3 & an attribute off: C4h, the attribute number, C4h\\
282: C6h & 6 & the end of an indented paragraph\\
283: \end{tabular}
284: 
285: \WPSide{Attribute Numbers}
286: \begin{tabular}{@{}rl@{\qquad}rl@{}}
287: 0 & extra large & 8 & italic\\
288: 1 & very large & 9 & shadow\\
289: 2 & large & 10 & redline\\
290: 3 & small & 11 & double underline\\
291: 4 & fine & 12 & bold\\
292: 5 & superscript & 13 & strikeout\\
293: 6 & subscript & 14 & underline\\
294: 7 & outline & 15 & small capitals\\
295: \end{tabular}
296: 
297: \WPSide{Variable-Length Codes}
298: \begin{tabular}{@{}llp{60mm}@{}}
299: \textbf{Code} & \textbf{Length} & \textbf{Meaning}\\
300: D0h 01h & 16 & left and right margins: the old and the new margins\\
301: D0h 02h & 12 & line spacing: the old and the new spacing, in 1/256 line\\
302: D0h 06h & 10 & justification: the old and the new, 0 left, 1 full, 2
303: center, 3 right\\
304: D4h 00h & 32 & the record WordPerfect keeps before each hard page\\
305: D4h 05h & 12 & the record of where a centered or right-justified line starts\\
306: \end{tabular}

308: \WPSide{Measurements}

310: measured from the left edge of the paper.
