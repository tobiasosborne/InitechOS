Tips

¢ If /Worksheet Global Recalculation is set to Manual, use /Copy to recalculate a
range when you have a worksheet containing many formulas and don’t want
to wait for 1-2-3 to recalculate the entire worksheet. If you copy a range to
itself (specify that range as both the TO and FROM range), 1-2-3 will
recalculate just the formulas in that range.

* To copy a range from another file to the current location of the cell pointer,
use /File Combine Copy.

* To copy a range and convert formulas to values, use /Range Value.

* To copy a column to a row or a row to a column, and convert formulas to
values, use /Range Trans.
