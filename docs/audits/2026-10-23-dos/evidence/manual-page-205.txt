Time                     Time
                         Purpose:
                         Allows you to enter or change the time known to the system.
                         Syntax:
                         time [hours:minutes[ ":Seconds [.hundredths]]]
                         Comments:
                         MS-DOS keeps track of time in a 24-hour format, and uses the
                         time information to update the directory whenever you create
                         or change a file.
