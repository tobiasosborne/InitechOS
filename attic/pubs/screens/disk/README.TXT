InitechDOS Version 3.30 -- Practice Diskette

This diskette contains the files used in the examples in
the InitechDOS User's Guide and the InitechDOS Reference.
You may copy, rename, and delete them as you work through
the examples.  The utility programs are in the directory
APPS; AUTOEXEC.BAT tells InitechDOS where to find them.

For assistance, call the Help Desk (extension 2504).
