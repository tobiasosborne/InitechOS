\WPHead{Getting Help}
InitechWord has no help screens: \K{F3}, the Help key of WordPerfect, shows
the word \textit{Help} in grey on the status line and nothing more.  Help
with InitechWord is in its publications and at the Initech Help Desk.

\WPSide{This Reference}
This binder is the reference for every feature of InitechWord.  It has four
sections, each behind a divider:
\begin{description}
\item[Getting Started] what you need to know before you begin, and a short
lesson.
\item[Reference] one entry for every feature, in alphabetical order.  An
entry begins with what the feature does, then gives the steps to use it.
Under \textit{Notes} it says how the feature works with others, and
\textit{See Also} names the entries that tell you more.
\item[Appendix] lists to look things up in: the characters, the codes, the
messages, the document file, the settings InitechWord starts with, the
keyboard, the menus and the limits.
\item[Glossary/Index] the words this book uses, and the index.
\end{description}
Entries are named the way you would look for them, the general word first:
the entry for a hard return is \textit{Return, Hard}, beside \textit{Return,
Soft}.  When one entry sends you to another it names the book as well, as in
``see \InRef{Block}.''

\WPSide{The Workbook}
The \textit{InitechWord Workbook} teaches InitechWord in lessons.  Each
lesson produces a real office document, and you type each keystroke as the
lesson gives it.  If you have not used a word processor before, work through
the lessons of the first section of the Workbook in order before you use
this Reference.

\WPSide{The Help Desk}
If you cannot find what you need in these publications, call the Initech
Help Desk at 555-0100, extension 2504.  Have the part number of your
InitechWord Reference beside you, and be at your computer with InitechWord
running when you call.  Help Desk support is available to registered users.
