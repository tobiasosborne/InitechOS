\chapter{Commands}
\Gates{test-pubs-commands, test-pubs-examples, test-samir-repl, test-interp-list}
This chapter describes every InitechBase command in alphabetical order.  The
symbol commands \cmd{?}, \cmd{??} and \cmd{@} come first.  Each command begins on a new
page.  The entry gives the purpose of the command, its syntax, its default
values, how it works, its options, practical tips, examples and the related
commands, in that order; a heading that does not apply to a command is left
out.

\ATEHead{Symbols and Conventions}
The syntax lines use the following symbols.  None of them is typed.

\noindent\hspace*{-\imHang}\begin{tabular}{@{}p{38mm}p{96mm}@{}}
\hline
\textsf{\textbf{Symbol}} & \textsf{\textbf{Use}}\\
\hline
\texttt{< >} & Enclose an item you supply, such as a field name, a file name or an
  expression.\\
\texttt{[ ]} & Enclose an item you may leave out.\\
\texttt{/} & Separates items of which you choose one.\\
\texttt{...} & The item before it may be repeated, separated by commas.\\
\xp{expC} & A character expression.\\
\xp{expN} & A numeric expression.\\
\xp{expD} & A date expression.\\
\xp{expL} & A logical expression.\\
\xp{exp} & An expression of any type.\\
\xp{expression list} & Expressions separated by commas.\\
\xp{condition} & A logical expression that each record is tested against.\\
\xp{scope} & The records a command works on: \kw{ALL}, \kw{NEXT} \xp{n},
  \kw{RECORD} \xp{n} or \kw{REST} (Chapter~2).\\
\xp{memvar} & The name of a memory variable.\\
\xp{file} & The name of a file, with a drive and an extension if needed.\\
\xp{alias} & The alias of a database open in another work area.\\
\texttt{*} & At the beginning of a line, a comment (\cmd{NOTE}).\\
\texttt{\&\&} & After a command, a comment that runs to the end of the line.\\
\hline
\end{tabular}
