% CLEAR -- real III+ screen-mint/at-clear-a CD, at-clear-b CG/CH, at-clear-d DA/DC/DD.
\begin{XbaseEntry}{CLEAR}{command}
Erases the whole screen.
\Syntax
\SyntaxLine{\kw{CLEAR} [\kw{ALL} | \kw{FIELDS} | \kw{GETS} | \kw{MEMORY} | \kw{TYPEAHEAD}]}
\Usage
With no word, the screen is blanked and the cursor goes to row 0, column 0.
CLEAR ALL closes the databases and releases all memory variables, and leaves the
screen as it was.  CLEAR TYPEAHEAD discards keys waiting to be read.  CLEAR GETS
and CLEAR FIELDS are accepted and have nothing to clear in this version.  Any
other word raises \xmsg{36}{Unrecognized phrase/keyword in command.}.
\SeeAlso{@, CLEAR MEMORY}
\end{XbaseEntry}
