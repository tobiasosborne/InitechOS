\chapter{Terminal Procedures}

This appendix summarizes the steps at the workstation.  The full procedures
are in Sessions 2 to 7.

\section{From the Desktop to the Program}
\begin{center}\small
\hspace*{-\imHang}%
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{50mm}p{\dimexpr\imFullWidth-50mm-2\tabcolsep\relax}@{}}
\rule{0pt}{10pt}\sffamily\bfseries To do this & \sffamily\bfseries Take these steps\\\hline
Leave the FLAIR desktop & Choose \textbf{Exit to InitechDOS} from the \textbf{Special} menu; click \textbf{Exit}\\
Keep the date and time & Press \key{Enter} at each prompt\\
Clear the screen & Type \type{CLS}, press \key{Enter}\\
Make the program directory current & Type \type{CD APPS}, press \key{Enter}\\
Check the program files & Type \type{DIR TPS*.* /W}, press \key{Enter}\\
Start the program & Type \type{TPSREP TPS.DBF}, press \key{Enter}\\\hline
\end{tabular}\end{center}

\section{Keys the Program Accepts}
At each step the program accepts only the keys below.  Any other key is
ignored and displays nothing.

\begin{center}\small
\hspace*{-\imHang}%
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{38mm}p{56mm}p{\dimexpr\imFullWidth-94mm-4\tabcolsep\relax}@{}}
\rule{0pt}{10pt}\sffamily\bfseries Step & \sffamily\bfseries Keys accepted & \sffamily\bfseries Displayed\\\hline
Form alignment (\proc{03}) & \key{Space Bar}; \key{Enter} ends the attempt & A period for each press\\
Report number (\proc{04}) & Digits (the hyphen is also accepted), up to six; \key{Enter} ends the entry & The characters\\
Memo number (\proc{05}) & Digits and the hyphen, up to eight; \key{Enter} ends the entry & The characters\\
Cover sheet (\proc{06}) & \type{Y} or \type{N}, capital or small & The letter, as a capital\\\hline
\end{tabular}\end{center}

\section{The Alignment Count}
The form is aligned by pressing the \key{Space Bar} exactly
\AlignCount{TPS-P-03} times, then \key{Enter}.  The program does not display
this number.
