\chapter{InitechPaint}
\label{ch:paint}
\index{InitechPaint}

InitechPaint opens a blank canvas in a document window, under a menu bar of
eight menus: \menuname{File}, \menuname{Edit}, \menuname{Image},
\menuname{Layer}, \menuname{Select}, \menuname{View}, \menuname{Window} and
\menuname{Help}.  In this version of FLAIR the canvas stays blank and every
command except \menuitem{Quit} is dimmed.

\section{Opening and quitting InitechPaint}
\Operation{FLAIR-DISPATCH}
\begin{TaskSteps}
\Step Open the \file{APPS} folder and double-click \file{PAINT.EXE}.
\Result{A canvas window titled \texttt{Untitled-1} opens at the right of
the screen, and the lower menu bar shows InitechPaint's eight menus.
InitechPaint's menu bar has no Apple menu; the Apple menu stays in the
upper bar.}
\end{TaskSteps}

\FlairScreen[scale=4, label=fig:paint]{a_paint}{InitechPaint}

To quit, choose \menuitem{Quit} from the \menuname{File} menu, press
\key{Ctrl-Q}, or click the close box of the canvas.
