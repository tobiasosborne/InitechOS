\Lesson{39}{Restoring Deleted Text}
\LessonFeatures{Undelete; Delete; Block, Delete; Cancel}
Everyone deletes the wrong thing now and then.  InitechWord keeps the last
three things you deleted, so that you can put them back where they were, or
somewhere else.  In this lesson you write a short note to the third floor,
change your mind twice, and use Undelete to recover.

\BeginReplay{l39}
\Task{Typing the Note}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Type \TypeRun{Third floor:} and press \PressKey{Enter} twice.\Keys{Enter}
\item Type \TypeRun{The cake will be served at three in the break room.}
and press \PressKey{Enter}.
\item Type \TypeRun{Please bring your own stapler.}
\end{WPSteps}

\Task{Restoring a Word}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \PressKey{Backspace} eight times\Keys{Backspace Backspace Backspace Backspace Backspace Backspace Backspace}
to erase \textit{stapler.}
\item Press \Press{Cancel}{F1}.  Nothing is showing to cancel, so
InitechWord offers to undelete: the word you erased appears again at the
cursor, highlighted.
\end{WPSteps}
\CompareScreen[rows=4]{l39-1}
\begin{WPSteps}[resume]
\item Select \Choose{Restore}{1}.  The word is back.
\end{WPSteps}

\Task{Moving a Line With Undelete}
The sentence about the stapler would be better before the one about the
cake.  You delete it, with the hard return in front of it, and put it back
in its new place.
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \PressKey{Up} and \PressKey{End} to go to the end of the line
about the cake.
\item Press \Press{Block}{Alt-F4}, then \PressKey{Home, Home, Down}.  The
block runs from the end of the cake line to the end of the note: the hard
return that ends the cake line and the whole stapler line.
\item Press \PressKey{Delete} and type \TypeName{y}.
\item Press \PressKey{Up} to go to the blank line under \textit{Third
floor:}.
\item Press \Press{Cancel}{F1}.  The deleted line is shown in its new
place.
\end{WPSteps}
\CompareScreen[rows=4]{l39-2}
\begin{WPSteps}[resume]
\item Select \Choose{Restore}{1}.
\end{WPSteps}
\CompareScreen[rows=4]{l39-3}
After a restore the cursor stays where it was, before the restored text.

\Task{Looking at an Earlier Deletion}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \Press{Cancel}{F1}.  The last deletion, the stapler line, is
shown.
\item Select \Choose{Previous Deletion}{2}.  The deletion before it,
\textit{stapler.}, is shown instead.
\item Press \Press{Cancel}{F1}.  Nothing is restored.
\item Press \Press{Exit}{F7}, type \TypeName{n} and \TypeName{n}.
\end{WPSteps}
\EndReplay
