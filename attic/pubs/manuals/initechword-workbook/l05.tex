\Lesson{5}{Phone List}
\LessonFeatures{Cursor Movement; Tab; Status Line; Save}
The third floor needs a list of telephone extensions.  The list is longer
than the screen, which makes it the right document for learning the keys
that move the cursor far.  In this lesson you type the list, then move
around it with the Home combinations.

\Task{Saving the List}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \Press{Save}{F10}, type \TypeName{phone} and press
\PressKey{Enter}.
\end{WPSteps}

\Task{Moving to the Top and the End}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \PressKey{Home}, \PressKey{Home}, \PressKey{Up}.  The cursor goes
to the top of the list, and the screen shows the top again.
\item Press \PressKey{Home}, \PressKey{Down}.  The cursor goes to the bottom
line of the screen, keeping its column.
\end{WPSteps}
\CompareScreen[rows=23]{l05-2}
\begin{WPSteps}[resume]
\item Press \PressKey{Home}, \PressKey{Down} again.  The cursor was already on
the bottom line, so it moves down a screen: here, to the end of the list.
\item Press \PressKey{Home}, \PressKey{Up}.  The cursor goes to the top line of
the screen.
\end{WPSteps}
\CompareScreen[rows=22]{l05-3}

\Task{Moving Along a Line}
\begin{WPSteps}[resume]
\item Press \PressKey{Down} to move to a line of the list, then press
\PressKey{End}: the cursor goes to the end of the line, after the extension.
\item Press \PressKey{Home}, \PressKey{Left}: the cursor goes back to the start
of the line.
\item Press \PressKey{Right} and watch Pos on the status line grow by a tenth
of an inch each time.  Press \PressKey{Right} again until the cursor reaches
the tab.  One press moves it over the whole tab.\Keys{Right Right Right Right Right Right Right Right Right Right Right Right Right}
\end{WPSteps}
\CompareScreen[rows=22]{l05-4}

\Task{Clearing the Screen}
\begin{WPSteps}[resume]
\item Press \Press{Exit}{F7}, then type \TypeName{n} and \TypeName{n}.  The
list was saved before you moved the cursor; moving the cursor does not
change a document, so there is nothing new to save.
\end{WPSteps}
