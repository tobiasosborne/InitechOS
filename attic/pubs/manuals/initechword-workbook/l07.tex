\Lesson{7}{Procedures}
\LessonFeatures{Indent; Return, Hard; Word Wrap; Save}
The cover sheets come with a procedure, and Bill wants it typed as a
numbered list.  The text of each step should line up to the right of its
number, even where it wraps.  Indent does that: it moves a whole paragraph
in to the next tab stop.  In this lesson you type the procedure and save it.

\BeginReplay{l07}
\Task{Typing the Title}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Type \TypeRun{COVER SHEET PROCEDURE} and press \PressKey{Enter}
twice.\Keys{Enter}
\end{WPSteps}

\Task{Typing the Steps}
For each step, type its number, press Indent, and type the text without
pressing Enter until the end of the step.
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Type \TypeRun{1.} and press \Press{\rarr Indent}{F4}.
\item Type
\Type{Take a cover sheet from the supply room on the third floor.}
\item Press \PressKey{Enter}.  Enter ends the step and its indent: the next
line starts at the left margin.
\item Type \TypeRun{2.}, press \PressKey{F4} and type
\Type{Fill in the report number, the date and your name. Print clearly, using a black pen.}
\item Press \PressKey{Enter}.
\end{WPSteps}
\CompareScreen[rows=6]{l07-1}
The second step is longer than a line.  Its second line wraps to the indent,
not to the margin, so the text stays clear of the number.
\begin{WPSteps}[resume]
\item Type \TypeRun{3.}, press \PressKey{F4} and type
\Type{Staple the cover sheet to the front of the report, in the top left corner.}
\item Press \PressKey{Enter}.
\item Type \TypeRun{4.}, press \PressKey{F4} and type
\Type{Put the report in the tray outside the office of Bill Lumbergh by four on Friday.}
\item Press \PressKey{Enter}.
\end{WPSteps}
\CompareScreen[rows=10]{l07-2}

\Task{Saving the Procedure}
\begin{WPSteps}[resume]
\item Press \Press{Save}{F10}, type \TypeName{procs} and press
\PressKey{Enter}.
\item Press \Press{Exit}{F7}, then type \TypeName{n} and \TypeName{n}.
\end{WPSteps}
\EndReplay
