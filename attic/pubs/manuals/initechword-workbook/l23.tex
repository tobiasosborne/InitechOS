\Lesson{23}{Business Letter}
\LessonFeatures{Retrieve; Word Wrap; Return, Hard; Save; Filenames}
Tom Smykowski asked what the new cover sheets are for.  In this lesson you
answer him on the letterhead you made in Lesson 18.  The letterhead already
holds the date and the salutation, so you type the body and the closing,
and save the letter under a name of its own.

\BeginReplay{l23}
\Task{Retrieving the Letterhead}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \Press{Retrieve}{Shift-F10}, type \TypeName{letterhd} and press
\PressKey{Enter}.
\item Press \PressKey{Home}, \PressKey{Home}, \PressKey{Down} to go to the end
of the letterhead, below the salutation.
\end{WPSteps}

\Task{Typing the Body}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Type the first paragraph:
\Type{Thank you for your question about the new cover sheets. Every TPS report now goes out with a cover sheet, so that each report can be traced to the person who prepared it and the date it was sent.}
\item Press \PressKey{Enter} twice.\Keys{Enter}
\item Type the second paragraph:
\Type{The cover sheets are in the supply room on the third floor. If you need more than a few, ask Milton Waddams in collating, who keeps the reserve stock.}
\item Press \PressKey{Enter} twice.\Keys{Enter}
\end{WPSteps}
\CompareScreen[rows=17]{l23-1}
The letterhead was typed with Full justification after the date, so the
body begins at the left margin and wraps at the right.

\Task{Typing the Closing}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Type \TypeRun{Sincerely,} and press \PressKey{Enter} four times to
leave room for the signature.\Keys{Enter Enter Enter}
\item Type \TypeRun{Bill Lumbergh} and press \PressKey{Enter}.
\item Type \TypeRun{Vice President}.
\end{WPSteps}
\CompareScreen[rows=22]{l23-2}
The whole letter fits on the screen.  Its last line is at \Scr{Ln 4.5"},
four and a half inches from the top of the page.

\Task{Saving the Letter}
The status line shows \Scr{A:\wbs LETTERHD}.  Save the letter under a new
name, so that the letterhead stays as it is for the next letter.
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \Press{Save}{F10}.  The status line offers \Scr{LETTERHD}.
\item Type \TypeName{smykowsk} and press \PressKey{Enter}.  A name has at
most eight characters before the period.
\item Press \Press{Exit}{F7}, type \TypeName{n} and \TypeName{n}.
\end{WPSteps}
\EndReplay
