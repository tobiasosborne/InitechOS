\Lesson{29}{Table of Figures}
\LessonFeatures{Tab; Reveal Codes; Save}
The supply room counts its stock every quarter.  In this lesson you type
the count as a small table, its columns lined up on tab stops two, three and
four inches in from the left margin.  How many tabs each figure needs
depends on how long the text before it is: a tab always goes to the next
stop after the cursor.

\BeginReplay{l29}
\Task{Typing the Table}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Type \TypeRun{SUPPLY ROOM COUNT, FIRST QUARTER} and press
\PressKey{Enter} twice.\Keys{Enter}
\item Type each line below: each cell, then the tabs shown, and Enter at the
end of the line.
\end{WPSteps}

\Task{Checking the Tabs}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \PressKey{Up} three times to go to the line of the staples, and
press \Press{Reveal Codes}{Alt-F3}.\Keys{Up Up}
\end{WPSteps}
\CompareScreen[rows=18]{l29-2}
Each \Code{[Tab]} moves the text after it to the next stop, so a short entry
needs more tabs than a long one to reach the same column.
\begin{WPSteps}[resume]
\item Press \Press{Reveal Codes}{Alt-F3}.
\end{WPSteps}

\PressKey{Enter}.
