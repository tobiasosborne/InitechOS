\Lesson{38}{Replacing Text}
\LessonFeatures{Replace; Retrieve; Save; Cursor Movement}
From next month the status reports go to Bill once a month, and the cover
sheet is to be called the cover page.  In this lesson you turn the weekly
status report of Lesson 31 into a monthly one by replacing words throughout
it, first one at a time and then all at once.

\BeginReplay{l38}
\Task{Replacing One at a Time}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \Press{Retrieve}{Shift-F10}, type \TypeName{status} and press
\PressKey{Enter}.
\item Press \Press{Replace}{Alt-F2}.  The status line asks
\Scr{w/Confirm? No (Yes)}.
\item Type \TypeName{y}: InitechWord is to ask you at each place.
\item At \Scr{-> Srch:}, type \TypeName{week} and press \PressKey{F2}.
\item At \Scr{Replace with:}, type \TypeName{month} and press
\PressKey{F2}.  InitechWord highlights the first place it finds and asks
whether to replace it.
\end{WPSteps}
\CompareScreen[rows=14]{l38-1}
\begin{Aside}\textit{week} in lower case finds \textit{WEEK} in capitals as
well, here inside \textit{WEEKLY}.\end{Aside}
\begin{WPSteps}[resume]
\item Type \TypeName{y}.  \textit{WEEKLY} becomes \textit{MONTHLY}: the new
word is given the capitals of the old.
\item Type \TypeName{y} again for \textit{Week ending}, and
\TypeName{y} for \textit{Done this week}.
\item The next place is \textit{the second week}, which is still right.
Type \TypeName{n} to leave it.
\item Type \TypeName{y} for \textit{Next week}.  There are no more, and the
status line shows \Scr{* Not found *}.
\end{WPSteps}
\CompareScreen[rows=14]{l38-2}

\Task{Replacing Everywhere at Once}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \PressKey{Enter} to clear the message, then press
\PressKey{Home, Home, Up}.
\item Press \Press{Replace}{Alt-F2} and type \TypeName{n}: InitechWord is
not to ask.
\item Type \TypeName{cover sheet} and press \PressKey{F2}.
\item Type \TypeName{cover page} and press \PressKey{F2}.
\end{WPSteps}
\CompareScreen[rows=14]{l38-3}
Both places are changed, \textit{cover sheets} included.

\Task{Saving the Monthly Report}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \PressKey{Enter} to clear the message.
\item Press \Press{Save}{F10}, type \TypeName{monthly} and press
\PressKey{Enter}.  The weekly report is still on the disk as
\Scr{STATUS}.
\item Press \Press{Exit}{F7}, type \TypeName{n} and \TypeName{n}.
\end{WPSteps}
\EndReplay
