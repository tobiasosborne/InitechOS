\Lesson{24}{Meeting Minutes}
\LessonFeatures{Bold; Indent; Page Break, Hard; Status Line; Save}
Minutes are a record of what was decided, set out so that a reader can find
each decision quickly.  In this lesson you type the minutes of the staff
meeting, with a bold heading for each part, the decisions indented, and the
action list on a page of its own.

% One action line: the name, the tabs, the action, Enter.
\newcommand\ActionLine[4]{\Typing{#1}\Keys{#2}\Typing{#4}\Keys{Enter}%
  \noindent\hspace*{4mm}\makebox[42mm][l]{\textbf{#1}}\makebox[18mm][l]{\textit{#3}}\textbf{#4}\par}
\BeginReplay{l24}
\Task{Typing the Heading}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \Press{Bold}{F6}, type \TypeRun{MINUTES OF THE STAFF MEETING},
and press \Press{Bold}{F6} again.
\item Press \PressKey{Enter}, type \TypeRun{Friday, March 13, 1992} and press
\PressKey{Enter} twice.\Keys{Enter}
\end{WPSteps}

\Task{Typing the Decisions}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \Press{Bold}{F6}, type \TypeRun{Decisions}, press
\Press{Bold}{F6} and press \PressKey{Enter} twice.\Keys{Enter}
\item Press \Press{\rarr Indent}{F4} and type:
\Type{The new cover sheet is required on every TPS report from Monday.}
\item Press \PressKey{Enter}, then \PressKey{F4} again and type:
\Type{Reports without a cover sheet will be returned to the sender.}
\item Press \PressKey{Enter}, \PressKey{F4}, and type:
\Type{The birthday cake for the third floor will be served at three.}
\item Press \PressKey{Enter}.
\end{WPSteps}
\CompareScreen[rows=11]{l24-1}

\Task{Starting the Action List on a New Page}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \PressKey{Ctrl-Enter}.  The bar of equal signs shows the end of
the first page, and the status line shows \Scr{Pg 2}.
\item Press \Press{Bold}{F6}, type \TypeRun{Action Items}, press
\Press{Bold}{F6} and press \PressKey{Enter} twice.\Keys{Enter}
\item Type each action: the name, \K{Tab} as many times as shown, the action,
and \K{Enter} at the end of the line.
\end{WPSteps}

\Task{Saving the Minutes}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \Press{Save}{F10}, type \TypeName{minutes} and press
\PressKey{Enter}.
\item Press \Press{Exit}{F7}, type \TypeName{n} and \TypeName{n}.
\end{WPSteps}
