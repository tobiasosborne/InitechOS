\Lesson{27}{Revising the Phone List}
\LessonFeatures{Move, Sentence/Paragraph/Page; Cursor Movement; Tab; Save}
Milton Waddams has moved to the basement, and a new person, Carl Adams, has
joined the third floor.  In this lesson you take Milton's line out of the
phone list and put Carl's in, in its place in the alphabet.

\BeginReplay{l27}
\Task{Deleting a Line}
Each line of the list ends with a hard return, so to InitechWord each line is
a paragraph, and Move can delete it whole.
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \Press{Retrieve}{Shift-F10}, type \TypeName{phone} and press
\PressKey{Enter}.
\item Press \PressKey{Home}, \PressKey{Home}, \PressKey{Down} to go to the end
of the list, and \PressKey{Up} to go to the last line, \textit{Waddams,
Milton}.
\item Press \Press{Move}{Ctrl-F4} and select \Choose{Paragraph}{2}.  The line
is highlighted, through its hard return.
\end{WPSteps}
\CompareScreen[rows=23]{l27-1}
\begin{WPSteps}[resume]
\item Select \Choose{Delete}{3}.  The line is gone.
\end{WPSteps}

\Task{Inserting a Line}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \PressKey{Home}, \PressKey{Home}, \PressKey{Up}, then
\PressKey{Down}\Keys{Down Down} three times to go to the start of the second
name of the list, \textit{Bolton, Michael}: \textit{Adams} comes after
\textit{Abernathy} and before \textit{Bolton}.
\item Type \TypeRun{Adams, Carl}, press \PressKey{Tab} three
times\Keys{Tab Tab}, type \TypeRun{125} and press \PressKey{Enter}.  The new
line goes in front of Bolton's, which moves down.
\end{WPSteps}
\CompareScreen[rows=23]{l27-2}

\Task{Saving the List}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \Press{Save}{F10}, press \PressKey{Enter}, and type
\TypeName{y} to replace the old list.
\item Press \Press{Exit}{F7}, type \TypeName{n} and \TypeName{n}.
\end{WPSteps}
\EndReplay
