\Lesson{34}{Two Attributes on a Block}
\LessonFeatures{Block, Attributes; Bold; Underline; Reveal Codes}
Giving a block an attribute turns Block off.  To give the same text a second
attribute, block it again.  In this lesson you make the first words of the
notice both bold and underlined, and look at the codes.

\BeginReplay{l34}
\Task{Making the Words Bold}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \Press{Retrieve}{Shift-F10}, type \TypeName{notice} and press
\PressKey{Enter}.
\item Press \PressKey{Down} twice to go to the line that begins
\textit{Effective immediately}.\Keys{Down}
\item Press \Press{Block}{Alt-F4}, then \PressKey{Right} twenty-one
times\Keys{Right Right Right Right Right Right Right Right Right Right Right Right Right Right Right Right Right Right Right Right}
to block \textit{Effective immediately,} with its comma.
\item Press \Press{Bold}{F6}.
\end{WPSteps}

\Task{Underlining the Same Words}
After Bold, the cursor is at the end of the words, after the bold code.
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \PressKey{Home}, \PressKey{Left} to go to the start of the line.
\item Press \Press{Block}{Alt-F4}, then \PressKey{Right} twenty-two times:
one more, to pass the bold code at the start.\Keys{Right Right Right Right Right Right Right Right Right Right Right Right Right Right Right Right Right Right Right Right Right}
\item Press \Press{Underline}{F8}.
\item Press \Press{Reveal Codes}{Alt-F3}.
\end{WPSteps}

\Task{Clearing the Screen}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \Press{Reveal Codes}{Alt-F3}.
\item Press \Press{Exit}{F7}, type \TypeName{n} and \TypeName{n}.
\end{WPSteps}
\EndReplay
