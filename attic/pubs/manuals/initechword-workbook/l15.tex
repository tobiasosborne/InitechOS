\Lesson{15}{Size}
\LessonFeatures{Size; Block, Attributes; Reveal Codes; Save}
The notice needs a large heading, and the approval line at its foot needs a
small raised reference mark.  Sizes are on the Font key's Size menu.  In
this lesson you give the heading the Large size by blocking it, and type a
superscript.

\BeginReplay{l15}
\Task{Making the Heading Large}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \Press{Retrieve}{Shift-F10}, type \TypeName{notice} and press
\PressKey{Enter}.  The cursor is at the start of the heading.
\item Press \Press{Block}{Alt-F4}, then \PressKey{End} to block the heading.
\item Press \Press{Font}{Ctrl-F8}.  With Block on, the status line shows
\Scr{Attribute: 1 Size; 2 Appearance: 0}.
\item Select \Choose{Size}{1}, then \Choose{Large}{5}.
\end{WPSteps}
The editing screen draws every size in the same cells, so the heading looks
as before; Reveal Codes will show the change.

\Task{Typing a Superscript}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \PressKey{Home}, \PressKey{Home}, \PressKey{Down} to go to the end
of the notice, and press \PressKey{Enter} twice.\Keys{Enter}
\item Type \TypeRun{Approved by the Office of the Vice President}.
\item Press \PressKC{Ctrl-F8}{1,1}{11} for superscript, type \TypeRun{1}, and
press \PressKC{Ctrl-F8}{1,1}{11} again.
\item Press \Press{Reveal Codes}{Alt-F3}.
\end{WPSteps}
\CompareScreen[rows=16]{l15-1}
The last line ends with \Code{[SUPRSCPT]1[suprscpt]}.  Press
\PressKey{Home}, \PressKey{Home}, \PressKey{Up} to see the codes around the
heading at the top:
\CompareScreen[rows=21]{l15-2}

\Task{Saving the Notice}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \Press{Reveal Codes}{Alt-F3}.
\item Press \Press{Save}{F10} and \PressKey{Enter}, and type \TypeName{y}.
\item Press \Press{Exit}{F7}, type \TypeName{n} and \TypeName{n}.
\end{WPSteps}
\EndReplay
