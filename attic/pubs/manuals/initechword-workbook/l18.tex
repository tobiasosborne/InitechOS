\Lesson{18}{Letterhead}
\LessonFeatures{Justification; Format; Reveal Codes; Save}
Initech letters begin with the company's name and address centered at the
top, and the date against the right margin.  In this lesson you make a
letterhead with Justification, and save it for use in Lesson 20.

\BeginReplay{l18}
\Task{Centering the Address}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \Press{Format}{Shift-F8}, select \Choose{Line}{1}, then
\Choose{Justification}{3}.  The bottom line shows the justifications.
\item Select \Choose{Center}{2}, and press \Press{Exit}{F7}.
\item Type each line of the address and press \K{Enter} after it; press it
twice after the last line:
\Type{Initech Systems Corporation}\Keys{Enter}
\Type{One Initech Plaza}\Keys{Enter}
\Type{Austin, Texas 78799}\Keys{Enter Enter}
\end{WPSteps}

\Task{Placing the Date}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \PressKC{Shift-F8}{1,3,3}{133} for Right justification, and press
\Press{Exit}{F7}.
\item Type \TypeRun{March 9, 1992} and press \PressKey{Enter} twice.\Keys{Enter}
\end{WPSteps}

\Task{Returning to Full Justification}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \PressKC{Shift-F8}{1,3,4}{134} for Full justification, the
justification of a new document, and press \Press{Exit}{F7}.
\item Type \TypeRun{Dear Mr. Smykowski:} and press \PressKey{Enter}
twice.\Keys{Enter}
\end{WPSteps}
\CompareScreen[rows=9]{l18-1}
The address is centered and the date ends at the right margin.  The
salutation starts at the left margin again.

\Task{Checking the Codes}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \PressKey{Home}, \PressKey{Home}, \PressKey{Up} and
\Press{Reveal Codes}{Alt-F3}.
\end{WPSteps}
\CompareScreen[rows=20]{l18-2}

\Task{Saving the Letterhead}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \Press{Reveal Codes}{Alt-F3}.
\item Press \Press{Save}{F10}, type \TypeName{letterhd} and press
\PressKey{Enter}.
\item Press \Press{Exit}{F7}, type \TypeName{n} and \TypeName{n}.
\end{WPSteps}
\EndReplay
In Lesson 19 you double-space a draft.
