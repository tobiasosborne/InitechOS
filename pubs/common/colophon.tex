\section*{Colophon}
This book is set with LuaLaTeX and the initech-manual class. The house faces
are TeX Gyre Termes and Pagella, Heros, Heros Cn, Adventor and Cursor, with
EB Garamond for display titles. The InitechDOS specimen uses Termes.
Screens are captured from InitechOS running in QEMU under the project
harness. They are decoded from emulator pixels, without redrawing.
\par
The publications factory and its correctness tools are C, make and thin
shell. LuaLaTeX is the ratified document-tool exception, decision D-11.
