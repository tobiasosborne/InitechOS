\begin{Feature}{Underline}
Underline draws a line under text.  On the editing screen underlined text is
drawn underlined.

To underline text as you type it,
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \FK{Underline}{F8}.  InitechWord puts the codes \Code{[UND]} and
\Code{[und]} in the document, with the cursor between them.
\item Type the text.
\item Press \FK{Underline}{F8} again to move past \Code{[und]}.
\end{WPSteps}
\Documents{wpkey}{F8}
\begin{WPNote}To underline text you have already typed, block it and press
Underline (see \InRef{Block, Attributes}).\end{WPNote}

\BeginReplay{ref-underline}
For example, type \TypeRun{Please read the } (with the space), press
\Press{Underline}{F8}, type \TypeRun{attached}, press \Press{Underline}{F8}
and type \TypeRun{ memo.}:
\WordScreen[rows=1]{ref-underline}{An underlined word}
\EndReplay

\Notes
\Topic{Double Underline}
For a double line, choose Dbl Und (3) from the Appearance menu of the Font
key (see \InRef{Appearance}).

\Topic{Spaces}
On the editing screen, the underline is drawn under everything between the
two codes, spaces included.

\Topic{Underline From the Font Menu}
Undln (2) on the Appearance menu of the Font key does what F8 does.

\SeeAlso{Appearance; Block, Attributes; Bold; Codes}
\end{Feature}

\begin{Feature}{Units of Measure}
InitechWord measures in inches.  The status line gives Ln and Pos in inches
from the top and the left edge of the paper, and you type margins in inches
(see \InRef{Margins, Left and Right}).  Line spacing is given in lines (see
\InRef{Line Spacing}).

\Notes
\Topic{Characters and Lines}
In the standard font, Courier 10 Pitch, a character is a tenth of an inch
wide, and a single-spaced line is a sixth of an inch high: so Pos grows by
0.1 inch with each character and Ln by 0.17 inch with each line.

\Topic{Inside the Document File}
In the document file, positions and margins are kept in WordPerfect units of
1/1200 inch (see Appendix D).

\Topic{Rounding}
Ln and Pos are shown to two decimal places at most, without trailing zeros:
\Scr{1.5"}, \Scr{1.17"}.

\SeeAlso{Margins, Left and Right; Status Line}
\end{Feature}
