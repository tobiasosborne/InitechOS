\begin{Feature}{Undelete}
Undelete puts back text you have deleted.  InitechWord keeps the last three
deletions, and shows each in turn at the cursor, so that you can choose the
one to restore.

To restore deleted text,
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Move the cursor to where the text is to go back.
\item Press \FK{Cancel}{F1} on the editing screen, with no menu or prompt
showing.  The status line shows \Scr{Undelete 1 Restore; 2 Previous
Deletion: 0}, and the last deletion appears at the cursor in reverse video.
\item Select \Ch{Restore}{1} to put that text back
\Or
Select \Ch{Previous Deletion}{2} to show the deletion before it, then
select \Ch{Restore}{1}.
\end{WPSteps}
\Documents{wpmenu}{F1,1=Restore}
\Documents{wpmenu}{F1,2=Previous Deletion}
The text is put back at the cursor, with the codes it held.

Press \PressKey{Backspace} eleven times\Keys{Backspace Backspace Backspace Backspace Backspace Backspace Backspace Backspace Backspace Backspace}
to erase \textit{department.}, then press \PressKey{Home, Left} and
\PressKey{Delete} eight times\Keys{Delete Delete Delete Delete Delete Delete Delete}

\WordScreen[rows=1]{ref-undelete-1}{The last deletion, shown at the cursor}
The deletion shown is the last one, \textit{Collate }.  Select

\WordScreen[rows=1]{ref-undelete-2}{The deletion before it}
Select \Choose{Restore}{1}: \textit{department.} is back at the end of the
line.
\EndReplay

\Notes
\Topic{Canceling}
Press \FK{Cancel}{F1} or \K{Esc} again to leave Undelete without restoring
anything.  The document is just as it was: the text shown in reverse video
was never in it.

\Topic{Codes}
Codes deleted with the text come back with it, except bold, underline and
the other appearance and size codes: those deleted with Backspace or Delete
are not kept.  A block deleted with Block and Delete, or with Move, comes
back whole, its codes included.

\Topic{Moving Through the Deletions}
At the Undelete prompt, \Ch{Previous Deletion}{2} and \K{Up} show the
deletion before the one shown, and \K{Down} the one after it.  Restore puts
back the one shown.

\Topic{Size of a Deletion}
A deletion holds up to 32K (32,768 bytes).  If you try to delete more at
once, as when deleting a very large block, the status line shows
\Scr{ERROR: Document too large} and nothing is deleted; delete the text in
smaller parts.

\Topic{What Is a Deletion}
Each character, code or block you delete becomes part of a deletion.
Characters deleted one after another at the same place with Backspace or
Delete make one deletion, so a word erased letter by letter comes back
whole.  Moving the cursor -- with the cursor keys or by searching -- ends a
deletion; the next thing you delete begins another.  When there are three
deletions and you delete more, the oldest is dropped.

\Topic{When Nothing Was Deleted}
If nothing has been deleted since InitechWord was started, Undelete shows
nothing at the cursor, and Restore does nothing.

\SeeAlso{Block, Delete; Cancel; Delete; Move}
\end{Feature}

\begin{Feature}{Underline}
Underline draws a line under text.  On the editing screen underlined text is
drawn underlined.

To underline text as you type it,
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \FK{Underline}{F8}.  InitechWord puts the codes \Code{[UND]} and
\Code{[und]} in the document, with the cursor between them.
\item Type the text.
\item Press \FK{Underline}{F8} again to move past \Code{[und]}.
\end{WPSteps}
\Documents{wpkey}{F8}
\begin{WPNote}To underline text you have already typed, block it and press
Underline (see \InRef{Block, Attributes}).\end{WPNote}

\Notes
\Topic{Double Underline}
For a double line, choose Dbl Und (3) from the Appearance menu of the Font
key (see \InRef{Appearance}).

\Topic{Spaces}
On the editing screen, the underline is drawn under everything between the
two codes, spaces included.

\Topic{Underline From the Font Menu}
Undln (2) on the Appearance menu of the Font key does what F8 does.

\SeeAlso{Appearance; Block, Attributes; Bold; Codes}
\end{Feature}

\begin{Feature}{Units of Measure}
InitechWord measures in inches.  The status line gives Ln and Pos in inches
from the top and the left edge of the paper, and you type margins in inches
(see \InRef{Margins, Left and Right}).  Line spacing is given in lines (see
\InRef{Line Spacing}).

\Notes
\Topic{Characters and Lines}
In the standard font, Courier 10 Pitch, a character is a tenth of an inch
wide, and a single-spaced line is a sixth of an inch high: so Pos grows by
0.1 inch with each character and Ln by 0.17 inch with each line.

\Topic{Inside the Document File}
In the document file, positions and margins are kept in WordPerfect units of
1/1200 inch (see Appendix D).

\Topic{Rounding}
Ln and Pos are shown to two decimal places at most, without trailing zeros:
\Scr{1.5"}, \Scr{1.17"}.

\Topic{Setup}
Setup's Units of Measure screen records a unit for margins and tabs and one
for the status line: inches, centimeters, points, 1200ths of an inch or
WordPerfect 4.2 units (see \InRef{Setup}).  InitechWord keeps your choice
and shows it on that screen, but in this release the status line and the
Format screens show inches, and margins are typed in inches, whatever the
choice.

\SeeAlso{Margins, Left and Right; Setup; Status Line}
\end{Feature}
