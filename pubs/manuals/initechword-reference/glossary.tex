\WPHead{Glossary}
\GlossTerm{Attribute}
A size or an appearance of text: bold, italic, large and so on.  Each is a
pair of codes around the text.

\GlossTerm{Block}
Text you mark with Block (Alt-F4) so that a feature acts on all of it.

\GlossTerm{Code}
A hidden mark in the document that formats the text: a hard return, a tab,
the start or end of bold.  Reveal Codes shows the codes.

\GlossTerm{Cursor}
The cell drawn in reverse video that marks where the next character you type
goes.

\GlossTerm{Data Volume}
The InitechOS disk on which InitechWord is kept and on which it saves your
documents.  InitechWord shows it as drive A:.

\GlossTerm{Default}
The setting InitechWord uses until you choose another (see Appendix E).

\GlossTerm{Deletion}
Text and codes erased together at one place, with Backspace, Delete or a
block deletion.  InitechWord keeps the last three deletions, so that
Undelete can put them back.

\GlossTerm{Document}
A letter, memo, report or other text you type in InitechWord.  Saved, it is
a file on the data volume.

\GlossTerm{Editing Screen}
The InitechWord window as you type: the document and the status line.

\GlossTerm{FLAIR}
The graphical environment of InitechOS, in which InitechWord runs.

\GlossTerm{Format}
The shape of the text on the page: its margins, justification and line
spacing.

\GlossTerm{Function Key}
One of the keys F1 to F12.  Alone or with Shift, Alt or Ctrl, each starts a
feature named on the template.

\GlossTerm{Grey Name}
The name of a WordPerfect feature InitechWord does not carry out, shown in
grey on the status line when you press its key and printed in grey on the
template.

\GlossTerm{Hard Page}
A page break you put in with Ctrl-Enter, as opposed to the end of a page
InitechWord counts after 54 lines.

\GlossTerm{Hard Return}
The end of a line you make by pressing Enter.

\GlossTerm{Justification}
Where lines sit between the margins: left, center, right or full.

\GlossTerm{Margin}
The space between an edge of the paper and the text.

\GlossTerm{Menu}
A list of numbered options on the status line or on a screen of its own, or
the File menu in the menu bar.

\GlossTerm{Move Buffer}
Where InitechWord keeps the text you last moved or copied, until you move or
copy other text.

\GlossTerm{Prefix Key}
A key that does nothing by itself but changes what the next key does.  Home
is a prefix key.

\GlossTerm{Prompt}
A question or a request for a name that InitechWord shows on the status
line.

\GlossTerm{Reveal Codes}
The divided screen that shows the codes of the document (Alt-F3).

\GlossTerm{Ruler}
The line of the Reveal Codes screen that shows the margins and the tab
stops.

\GlossTerm{Search String}
The text and codes Search or Replace looks for, typed at the \Scr{Srch:}
prompt.  InitechWord keeps it for the next search.

\GlossTerm{Setup}
The screens on which InitechWord records its settings, such as the units of
measure, in the file \Scr{IWORD.SET}.

\GlossTerm{Soft Return}
The end of a line InitechWord makes when a line is full.

\GlossTerm{Status Line}
The bottom line of the InitechWord window.

\GlossTerm{Tab Stop}
A place a tab moves the text to: every half inch from the left margin.

\GlossTerm{Template}
The card that lies above the function keys and names their features.

\GlossTerm{WPU}
WordPerfect unit, 1/1200 of an inch: the unit of measure inside an
InitechWord document file.

\GlossTerm{Word Wrap}
InitechWord's moving of a word that does not fit to the next line.
