\newcommand\ColophonScreens{The screen figures in this book were captured from
InitechOS running in an emulator under the project's test harness, driven by a
recorded list of keystrokes; each is the window's own pixels converted to grey
through a fixed table, placed at three printer dots a pixel.  The screen panels
of the examples and of A Brief Lesson are the cells the shipped InitechWord
program drew when the build replayed the keystrokes the book prints, on the
publications host, through a recording replacement of the FLAIR Toolbox;
the build replays every one of them again before the book is printed.}
