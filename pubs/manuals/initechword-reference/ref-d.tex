\begin{Feature}{Delete}
You erase text with two keys: \K{Backspace} erases the character to the
left of the cursor, and \K{Delete} erases the character at the cursor.
Text after the cursor moves up to close the gap, and the paragraph is
wrapped again.

To delete text,
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Move the cursor just after the character you want to erase and press
\K{Backspace}
\Or
Move the cursor onto the character and press \K{Delete}.
\item Hold the key down to erase several characters in a row.
\end{WPSteps}
\Documents{wpkey}{Backspace}
\Documents{wpkey}{Delete}
\begin{WPNote}To delete more than a few characters, block them and press
Delete (see \InRef{Block, Delete}), or have Move delete the sentence,
paragraph or page (see \InRef{Move, Sentence/Paragraph/Page}).\end{WPNote}

\Notes
\Topic{Codes}
Backspace and Delete erase codes as they erase characters, one at a time.
Some codes are not erased at once on the editing screen: InitechWord asks
first (see \InRef{Codes}).

\Topic{Joining Paragraphs}
A hard return is erased like a character.  Erase the hard return at the end
of a paragraph -- with Delete at the end of its last line, or with
Backspace at the start of the next paragraph -- and the two paragraphs
become one.

\Topic{Shift, Ctrl and Alt}
Backspace and Delete erase one character whatever Shift, Ctrl or Alt you
hold with them.

\Topic{The Start and End of the Document}
Backspace at the very start of the document and Delete at its very end do
nothing.
