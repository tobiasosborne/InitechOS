\doctitle{Learning InitechBase} teaches you to use InitechBase by using it.
Each chapter is a lesson that you work through at your computer, typing the
commands as the steps tell you and comparing what you see with the panels in
the book.  You need no knowledge of databases or of programming.

The lessons use the staff roster of Initech Systems Corporation, kept in the
database \file{STAFF.DBF} on the Sample Programs and Utilities diskette, with
the list of departments in \file{DEPTS.DBF}.  You will list the employees,
find them, change their records, total their salaries in a report and, in the
last chapter, run a program that does a task for you.

\section{Before You Begin}
Install InitechBase and copy the sample diskette as \doctitle{Getting
Started} describes, and learn there how to start InitechBase and how to leave
it.  Do the lessons in order: each assumes what the ones before it taught.
Allow about an hour for each.

Each lesson begins with the files as they are on the sample diskette.  Some
lessons change the files; before you begin a lesson, copy the files of the
diskette again, so that your screen agrees with the book.

\section{How This Section Is Organized}
\SideHead{Chapter 1}Quick Start: open a database, look at its records, ask
InitechBase a question, and leave.

\SideHead{Chapter 2}Looking at Your Data: choose the fields and the records
that \cmd{LIST} shows.

\SideHead{Chapter 3}Moving Through a Database: the record pointer and the
commands that move it.

\SideHead{Chapter 4}Finding Records: search with \cmd{LOCATE}, and find at once
with an index.

\SideHead{Chapter 5}Changing Your Data: add, change, delete and remove
records.

\SideHead{Chapter 6}Calculating: memory variables, arithmetic, dates and
functions.

\SideHead{Chapter 7}Reports: print a report with a report form.

\SideHead{Chapter 8}Working with Several Databases: work areas, aliases and
filters.

\SideHead{Chapter 9}From Commands to Programs: run a program, and read it.

\section{Conventions Used in This Section}
The steps of each lesson are numbered.  What you type is printed in bold:
type it exactly as shown, then press \key{Enter}.  The reply of InitechBase
follows the step.  The shaded panels show your screen as it is at the end of
the steps before them.
