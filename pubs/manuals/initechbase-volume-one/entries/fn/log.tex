\begin{XbaseEntry}{LOG}{function}
Returns the natural logarithm of a numeric expression, as a numeric value.
\Syntax
\SyntaxLine{\kw{LOG(}\xp{expN}\kw{)}}
\Usage
\fn{LOG()} returns the natural logarithm (base \texttt{e}) of its argument.
The argument must be greater than zero. With zero or a negative argument,
the numeric result displays as asterisks; InitechBase completes the statement
before raising \xmsg{58}{LOG() : Zero or negative.}.
An argument that is not numeric, or a call with other than one argument,
raises \xmsg{11}{Invalid function argument.}.
