\FrontChapter{How to Use This Guide}
\index{guide, how to use}
This guide describes the accessories and applications that come with
FLAIR\tm{} on the InitechOS\tm{} data disk and have no book of their own:

\begin{itemize}
\item the Apple menu and About This Computer (Chapter~1);
\item the Calculator, a desk accessory (Chapter~2);
\item InitechText, a text editor for memos and notes (Chapter~3);
\item InitechCalc\tm{}, the ledger and chart of the quarterly results
  (Chapter~4);
\item InitechPaint\tm{} (Chapter~5);
\item the two sample applications, C Tenant and TenantFix (Chapter~6).
\end{itemize}
Chapter~7 tells you where to find the other programs on the data disk, and
Chapter~8 puts the accessories to work on a small task.
Appendix~A lists the messages these applications display, Appendix~B their
keys, and the Glossary the terms this guide uses.

Each chapter stands on its own: read the chapters for the applications you
use.  The guide assumes that you know how to use the mouse, choose
commands, and open and close windows and applications, as described in Part
I of the \doctitle{FLAIR User's Guide} (1031-0000-10).  If you are new to
FLAIR, work through that part first.

\section{Conventions Used in This Guide}
\SideHead{Steps}Tasks are written as numbered steps.  The step itself is in
bold type; the plain text under it tells you what happens.

\SideHead{Menu commands}Commands and menus are in bold type: ``Choose
\menuitem{Save} from the \menuname{File} menu.''  The command is spelled
exactly as it appears in the menu.

\SideHead{Keys}Key names are shown like this: \key{Ctrl}, \key{Enter}.
\key{Ctrl-S} means: hold down \key{Ctrl}, press \key{S}, and release both.
A menu shows such a keyboard equivalent as a caret and the letter,
\CmdKey{S}.

\SideHead{What you type}Text that you type is shown in this typeface:
\type{12+30=}.

\SideHead{Screens}The figures are reproductions of the screen exactly as
it appeared when the task was carried out, with the clock set to 9:30 in
the morning.
