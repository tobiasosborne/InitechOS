\chapter{Control Panels, Note Pad and Scrapbook}
\label{ch:moreacc}
\index{Control Panels}\index{Note Pad}\index{Scrapbook}

Three more accessories are in the Apple menu of the data disk as delivered.
Like the Calculator, each is listed only while its program is in the
\file{APPS} folder, and none opens while another application from the disk
is open.

\section{Control Panels}
Choose \menuitem{Control Panels} from the Apple menu.  A window lists five
panels: \texttt{Date \& Time}, \texttt{Desktop Patterns}, \texttt{Mouse},
\texttt{Sound} and \texttt{Memory}.  Click the name of a panel to open it.
Every panel but \texttt{Memory} has an \texttt{OK} button, which keeps what
you set and saves it on the disk in the file \file{CPANELS.DAT}, and a
\texttt{Cancel} button, which puts everything back as it was.  If FLAIR
cannot save the settings, the panel says \texttt{Settings could not be
saved.}

\SideHead{Date \& Time}Sets the hour and minute, the day, the month and the
year with arrow buttons, and has a \texttt{Menu Bar Clock} choice that shows
or hides the clock at the right of the menu bar.

\SideHead{Desktop Patterns}Nine choices for the pattern of the desktop:
\texttt{Teal solid}, \texttt{Black}, \texttt{Dark stipple}, \texttt{Stipple},
\texttt{Gray}, \texttt{Rows}, \texttt{Columns}, \texttt{Diagonal} and
\texttt{Lines}.

\SideHead{Mouse}\texttt{Tracking Speed} and \texttt{Double-Click Speed}, each
\texttt{Slow}, \texttt{Normal} or \texttt{Fast}.

\SideHead{Sound}\texttt{Alert Sound} (\texttt{Beep}, \texttt{Bell} or
\texttt{Click}) and \texttt{Beep Length} (\texttt{Silent}, \texttt{Short} or
\texttt{Full}), with a \texttt{Test} button.

\SideHead{Memory}Shows \texttt{Built-in Memory}, \texttt{System},
\texttt{Largest Unused Block} and \texttt{Disk Cache}, in kilobytes, and
\texttt{Virtual Memory: Off}.  Nothing in this panel can be changed.

\section{Note Pad}
Note Pad keeps up to eight pages of text, each of at most 1024 characters,
in the file \file{NOTEPAD.DAT}.  Type into the page in front; Note Pad asks
whether to save before it closes.

\section{Scrapbook}
Scrapbook keeps up to 32 pieces of text, each of at most 1024 characters, in
the file \file{SCRAPBK.DAT}.  It keeps text only.  When it is full, or a
piece is too large, it says \texttt{The Scrapbook is full, or this clip is
too large.}
