\chapter{InitechCalc}
\index{InitechCalc}

InitechCalc keeps the quarterly ledger and draws its pie chart.  It opens
with the ledger already filled in, so that you can check the figures,
change them, and save the tables to the disk.

\section{Opening InitechCalc}
\begin{TaskSteps}
\Step Open the \file{APPS} folder and double-click \file{CALC.EXE}.
\Result{InitechCalc opens two windows: \texttt{InitechCalc}, the ledger,
at the left, and \texttt{Pie Chart} at the right.  The lower menu bar shows
its menus: the Apple menu and \menuname{File}.}
\end{TaskSteps}

\FlairScreen[scale=4, label=fig:icalc]{a_icalc}{InitechCalc: the ledger
and the pie chart}

\section{The ledger}
\index{InitechCalc!ledger}
The ledger has two columns, \texttt{A} and \texttt{B}, and three rows of
entries: an item in column A and its amount in column B.  Below them are:

\SideHead{The minus format}A heading and an amount that show how the
ledger writes a negative amount: with its minus sign at the end,
\texttt{570-}.  Every negative amount in the ledger is written this way.

\SideHead{The entry line}The line on which what you type appears before you
place it in a cell.

\SideHead{The total}The total of the quarter, \texttt{\$112,276.87}.

\draft{the amounts of the three ledger rows, and the empty entry line,
show the placeholder TODO\_FRAME until their values are locked
(spec/assets/calc\_ledger.json)}

\section{Changing an amount}
\index{InitechCalc!entering}
The \term{cell pointer}, a black line above a cell, shows which cell you are
working in.  It starts at the first row of column A.
\begin{TaskSteps}
\Step Press \key{Right Arrow} to move the cell pointer to column B.
\Step Type the new amount, for example \type{1200}.
\Result{What you type appears on the entry line.  Press \key{Backspace} to
correct it, or \key{Esc} to start again.}
\Step Press \key{Tab}.
\Result{The amount takes its place in the cell, and the entry line is
cleared.}
\end{TaskSteps}

\FlairScreen[scale=4, label=fig:icalcentry]{a_icalcentry}{1200 entered in
the first row of column B}

\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{38mm}p{61mm}@{}}
\textbf{Press} & \textbf{To}\\\hline
\key{Left Arrow} & place the entry and move to column A\\
\key{Right Arrow}, \key{Tab} & place the entry and move to column B\\
\key{Up Arrow}, \key{Down Arrow} & place the entry and move to the row
above or below\\
\key{Backspace} & erase the last character of the entry\\
\key{Esc} & abandon the entry\\
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
An entry that is a number, such as \type{1200} or \type{-570}, is placed as
a number, and a negative number is shown with its minus sign at the end; any
other entry is placed as text.

\FlairScreen[scale=4, label=fig:icalcneg]{a_icalcneg}{-570 typed in the
second row of column B, shown as 570-}  The cell pointer moves within the three
rows of the two columns.

\draft{InitechCalc ends an entry on a carriage return, and the keyboard
sends Enter as a line feed: pressing Enter does not place the entry in this
version (calc.c, event.c)}

\section{The pie chart}
\index{InitechCalc!pie chart}\index{pie chart}
The \texttt{Pie Chart} window shows the quarter's five shares as the
slices of a pie, shaded so that neighbouring slices differ, with the share
of each slice beside it: 40\%, 35\%, 18\%, 14\% and 9\%.  Below them is
their total, 116\%.

\FlairScreen[scale=4, label=fig:pie]{a_pie}{The Pie Chart window}

The chart is drawn from the shares InitechCalc keeps with its ledger; it
does not change when you change an amount in the ledger.

\section{Saving the tables}
\index{InitechCalc!saving}\index{FILE COPY}
\Operation{TENANT-FILECOPY}
\begin{TaskSteps}
\Step Choose \menuitem{Save} from the \menuname{File} menu, or press
\key{Ctrl-S}.
\Result{The \texttt{FILE COPY} box appears in the middle of the screen,
with the message \texttt{Saving tables to disk...} and a progress bar.  The
bar fills from left to right as InitechCalc copies its ledger file,
\file{LEDGER.TXT}, to the file \file{TABLES.TXT} in the \file{APPS}
folder.}
\end{TaskSteps}

\FlairScreen[scale=4, label=fig:filecopy]{a_filecopy}{Saving tables to
disk: the FILE COPY box half way}

While the box is on the screen the computer is busy with the copy: clicks
and keys are ignored, and the box cannot be moved or cancelled.  When the
bar is full the box goes away by itself and you can go on working.

The speed of the bar is set by the file \file{MICHAEL.CFG} in the
\file{APPS} folder, whose line \file{PROGRESS\_BAR\_RATE=20} makes the bar
fill 20 per cent of its length each second, so that a save takes five
seconds.  Each save writes \file{TABLES.TXT} afresh, replacing the copy
made by the save before.

\section{Quitting InitechCalc}
Choose \menuitem{Quit} from the \menuname{File} menu, press \key{Ctrl-Q},
or click the close box of either window.  InitechCalc quits at once, and
both its windows close.  The amounts you typed are not kept: the ledger
opens with its own figures the next time.

\FlairScreen[scale=4, label=fig:icalcfile]{a_icalcfile}{InitechCalc's File

\end{MenuReference}
