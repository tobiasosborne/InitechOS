\chapter{Other Programs on the Data Disk}
\label{ch:programs}

The \file{APPS} folder holds more programs than the ones this guide
describes.  Each has its own book.

\SideHead{Initech 123}\file{123.EXE} with its menu file \file{123.MNU}: the
spreadsheet.  Double-click \file{123.EXE} to open it.  See the
\doctitle{Initech 123 Reference}.

\SideHead{InitechWord}\file{WORD.EXE}: the word processor.  Double-click it
to open it.  See the \doctitle{InitechWord Reference}.

\SideHead{InitechDOS programs}\file{ATTRIB.COM}, \file{CHKDSK.COM},
\file{EDLIN.COM}, \file{LABEL.COM}, \file{TREE.COM} and \file{XCOPY.COM}.
These run at the InitechDOS command prompt, not on the desktop: choose
\menuitem{Exit to InitechDOS} from the Finder's \menuname{Special} menu,
change to the folder with \type{CD APPS}, and type the program's name.  See
the \doctitle{InitechDOS Reference}.

\SideHead{TPS Report Generator}\file{TPSREP.COM}, with its settings file
\file{TPSPARM.DAT} and its database \file{TPS.DBF}.  It runs at the
InitechDOS command prompt.  See \doctitle{TPS Report Generator Operating
Procedures}.

\section{Files the accessories use}
\index{files used by the accessories}
\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{28mm}p{71mm}@{}}
\textbf{File} & \textbf{Used by}\\\hline
\file{CALCULAT.EXE} & the Calculator's program; while it is in \file{APPS},

\file{TEXT.EXE} & InitechText's program\\
\file{CALC.EXE} & InitechCalc's program\\

\file{TABLES.TXT} & the copy InitechCalc writes when you save its tables;

\file{CTENANT.EXE}, \file{TENANTFX.EXE} & the sample applications\\
\end{tabular}

Leave these files in the \file{APPS} folder.  The Calculator is listed in
the Apple menu only while \file{CALCULAT.EXE} is there (Chapter~\ref{ch:apple}), and
InitechCalc saves its tables by copying \file{APPS\textbackslash
LEDGER.TXT} to \file{APPS\textbackslash TABLES.TXT}.

Appendix~A of the \doctitle{FLAIR User's Guide} lists every file on the
data disk.
