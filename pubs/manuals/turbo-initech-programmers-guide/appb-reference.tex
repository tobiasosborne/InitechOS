\section{Token kinds}

kind of token as follows.  In the lexical listing the reserved words appear
as \code{KW} followed by the word in small letters (Chapter 1); in a
\code{found=} or \code{expected=} field of a syntax error they appear in this
form.

\section{The ASCII character set}
\ix{ASCII}

codes 32 through 126.  \std{chr}{Chr} and \std{ord}{Ord} convert between
