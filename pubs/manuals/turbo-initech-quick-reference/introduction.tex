\IntroductionChapter

This booklet summarizes the Turbo Initech\reg{} language, the standard
routines, the compiler and its messages.  Each item gives the form in
bold, then what it does.  The full rules are in the \doctitle{Programmer's
Guide} (1077-0000-11), the routines in the \doctitle{Library Reference}
(1077-0000-12), and the use of the compiler in the \doctitle{User's Guide}
(1077-0000-10).

Words in \textit{italics} stand for what you supply.  Brackets
\code{[ ]} enclose what may be left out, and \code{...} marks what may be
repeated.  Program text is shown in \texttt{Cursor}; reserved words are
in \textbf{bold}.
