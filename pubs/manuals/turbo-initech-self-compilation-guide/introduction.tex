\IntroductionChapter

Turbo Initech\reg{} is written in the language it compiles.  This book
describes how the first Turbo Initech compiler is made from its Pascal
source, how every later compiler is made by the compiler before it, and
how a compiler proves that it reproduces itself exactly.  It is written for
the people who build and accept Turbo Initech: release engineers,
maintainers of the compiler, and auditors of its builds.

Chapter 1 explains the bootstrap.  Chapter 2 describes the genesis
compiler, the program on the development system that makes the first
compiler.  Chapter 3 describes the build of Turbo Initech and of the
programs it compiles.  Chapter 4 is the acceptance procedure, step by step,
in the form in which it is carried out and recorded.  Chapters 5 and 6
describe the further checks: compilation by an independent compiler, and
the rules that keep every build reproducible.  Appendix A lists the
programs that every build of Turbo Initech must compile correctly.

\section{Typefaces used in this book}
\ix{typefaces}
In addition to the typefaces of the other books, this book shows the
commands typed on the development system after a dollar-sign prompt,
\code{\$ \textbf{make test-compiler}}, and what they print in the same
typeface as program output.
