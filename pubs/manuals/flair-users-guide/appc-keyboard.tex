\chapter{Keyboards and Characters}
\index{keyboard}\index{characters}

FLAIR reads the Initech 101-key keyboard with the United States layout.
The characters you can type in FLAIR's applications are the 95 printable
characters of the ASCII set, less the two on the backslash key.

\section{Characters}
\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{30mm}p{69mm}@{}}
\textbf{Characters} & \textbf{Keys}\\\hline
\texttt{a} to \texttt{z} & the letter keys\\
\texttt{A} to \texttt{Z} & \key{Shift} and a letter key, or a letter key
with \key{Caps Lock} on\\
\texttt{0} to \texttt{9} & the top row, or the numeric keypad\\
\texttt{- = [ ] ; ' ` , . /} & the punctuation keys\\
\texttt{! @ \# \$ \% \^{} \& * ( ) \_ +} & \key{Shift} and a key of the top
row\\
\texttt{\{ \} : " \~{} < > ?} & \key{Shift} and a punctuation key\\
\texttt{+ - * .} & the keys of the numeric keypad\\
space & the space bar\\
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
The key marked with the backslash and the vertical bar is reserved for
InitechBase (Chapter~\ref{ch:keyboard}): neither character can be typed in a FLAIR
application.

\section{Other keys}
\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{30mm}p{69mm}@{}}
\textbf{Key} & \textbf{In FLAIR}\\\hline
\key{Enter} & ends a line; chooses the default button of an alert or
dialog box.  The \key{Enter} key of the numeric keypad does the same\\
\key{Backspace} & erases the character before the insertion point\\
\key{Tab} & used by some applications (InitechCalc)\\
\key{Esc} & chooses \texttt{Cancel} in an alert or dialog box\\
arrow keys & move the insertion point, a cell pointer or a list selection\\
\key{Ctrl} & held down with a letter, chooses a menu command\\
\key{Shift} & capitals; with a click, adds to the selection\\
\key{Caps Lock} & capitals until you press it again\\
\key{Alt} & not used by FLAIR\\
\key{F1} to \key{F12} & not used by FLAIR; InitechWord and Initech 123 use
them\\
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
