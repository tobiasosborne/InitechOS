\section{Commands that act}
\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{28mm}p{13mm}p{8mm}p{41mm}@{}}
\textbf{Command} & \textbf{Menu} & \textbf{Key} & \textbf{Available when}\\\hline
About This Computer... & Apple & & always\\
Calculator & Apple & & \file{CALCULAT.EXE} is on the disk and no other
application from the disk is open\\
New Folder & File & \CmdKey{N} & a Finder window is in front (always
drawn available)\\
Open & File & \CmdKey{O} & a folder is selected in the front Finder window\\
Close Window & File & \CmdKey{W} & a Finder window is in front\\
Select All & Edit & \CmdKey{A} & always\\
by Icons & View & & a Finder window is in front\\
Clean Up & View, Special & & a Finder window is in front\\
Arrange (by Name) & View & & a Finder window is in front\\
Empty Trash & Special & & the Trash holds something\\
Restart & Special & & always\\
Shut Down & Special & & always\\
Exit to InitechDOS & Special & & always\\
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Every other command of the Finder's menus is dimmed in this version of
FLAIR; Chapter~8 lists them.

\section{Actions without a command}
\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{40mm}p{59mm}@{}}
\textbf{To} & \textbf{Do this}\\\hline
open an icon & double-click it\\
move an item to a folder & drag it onto the folder or into its window\\
move an item to the top level & drag it onto the \texttt{INITECH} icon\\
throw an item away & drag it onto the Trash\\
take an item out of the Trash & drag it out of the Trash window\\
move a window & drag its title bar\\
change a window's size & drag its size box\\
fill the screen with a window & click its zoom box\\
roll a window up & click its collapse box\\
close a window & click its close box\\
bring a window to the front & click it\\
select several icons & Shift-click them, or drag a rectangle round them\\
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
