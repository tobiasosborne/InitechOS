\Lesson{30}{Copying Between Documents}
\LessonFeatures{Move, Block; Move, Retrieve; Move, Sentence/Paragraph/Page; Retrieve; Exit}
The text you copy stays in InitechWord's Move buffer until you copy or move
something else, even when you clear the screen and retrieve another
document.  In this lesson you copy a step of the procedure and put it at the
end of the memo.

\BeginReplay{l30}
\Task{Copying the Step}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \Press{Retrieve}{Shift-F10}, type \TypeName{procs} and press
\PressKey{Enter}.
\item Press \PressKey{Down} five times to go to the line that begins with
\textit{3.}\Keys{Down Down Down Down}
\item Press \Press{Move}{Ctrl-F4}, select \Choose{Paragraph}{2} and
\Choose{Copy}{2}.  The status line shows \Scr{Move cursor; press Enter to
retrieve.}
\item Press \Press{Cancel}{F1}.  The copy stays in the Move buffer.
\item Press \Press{Exit}{F7}, type \TypeName{n} and \TypeName{n}: the screen
clears.
\end{WPSteps}

\Task{Putting the Step in the Memo}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \Press{Retrieve}{Shift-F10}, type \TypeName{memo} and press
\PressKey{Enter}.
\item Press \PressKey{Home}, \PressKey{Home}, \PressKey{Down} to go to the end
of the memo, and press \PressKey{Enter} twice.\Keys{Enter}
\item Press \Press{Move}{Ctrl-F4}, select \Choose{Retrieve}{4} and
\Choose{Block}{1}.  The step from the procedure is put in at the cursor,
with its indent.
\end{WPSteps}
\CompareScreen[rows=21]{l30-1}

\Task{Clearing the Screen}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \Press{Exit}{F7}, type \TypeName{n} and \TypeName{n}.  The memo
on the disk is unchanged.
\end{WPSteps}
\EndReplay
