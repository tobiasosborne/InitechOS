\Lesson{16}{Normal}
\LessonFeatures{Font; Bold; Underline; Reveal Codes}
When text has several attributes at once -- bold and underlined, say --
ending them one by one takes a keystroke each.  Normal, on the Font menu,
ends them all.  In this lesson you type a bold, underlined word and go back
to normal text with one choice.

\BeginReplay{l16}
\Task{Typing the Word}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \Press{Bold}{F6} and \Press{Underline}{F8}.
\item Type \TypeRun{URGENT:}
\item Press \Press{Font}{Ctrl-F8} and select \Choose{Normal}{3}.  The cursor
moves past the codes that end the underline and the bold.
\item Type \TypeRun{ the staff meeting moves to Conference Room B.} (with the
space at the start).
\end{WPSteps}
\CompareScreen[rows=1]{l16-1}

\Task{Checking the Codes}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \Press{Reveal Codes}{Alt-F3}.
\end{WPSteps}
\CompareScreen[rows=13]{l16-2}
The word is inside \Code{[BOLD][UND]} \ldots\ \Code{[und][bold]}, and the
text after it is plain.

\Task{Clearing the Screen}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \Press{Reveal Codes}{Alt-F3}.
\item Press \Press{Exit}{F7}, type \TypeName{n} and \TypeName{n}.
\end{WPSteps}
\EndReplay
