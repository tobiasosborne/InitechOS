\Lesson{25}{Final Copy}
\LessonFeatures{Reveal Codes; Line Spacing; Codes; Move, Sentence/Paragraph/Page; Save}
The draft of Lesson 19 has come back without changes, so it can go out.  The
final copy is single-spaced and does not need the request for comments.  In
this lesson you remove the double spacing by deleting its code, delete the
request, and save the final copy beside the draft.

\BeginReplay{l25}
\Task{Removing the Double Spacing}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \Press{Retrieve}{Shift-F10}, type \TypeName{draft} and press
\PressKey{Enter}.
\item Press \Press{Reveal Codes}{Alt-F3}.  The cursor is at the very top of
the draft, on the \Code{[Ln Spacing:2]} code.
\end{WPSteps}
\CompareScreen[rows=17]{l25-1}
\begin{WPSteps}[resume]
\item Press \PressKey{Delete}.  In Reveal Codes the code is deleted without a
question, and the draft is single-spaced from the top.
\item Press \Press{Reveal Codes}{Alt-F3}.
\end{WPSteps}
\CompareScreen[rows=4]{l25-2}

\Task{Deleting the Request}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \PressKey{Home}, \PressKey{Home}, \PressKey{Down} to go to the end,
in the line \textit{Please return your comments by Wednesday.}
\item Press \Press{Move}{Ctrl-F4}, select \Choose{Sentence}{1} and
\Choose{Delete}{3}.
\end{WPSteps}
\CompareScreen[rows=4]{l25-3}

\Task{Saving the Final Copy}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \Press{Save}{F10}, type \TypeName{final} and press
\PressKey{Enter}.  The draft stays on the disk as it was; the final copy is a
document of its own.
\item Press \Press{Exit}{F7}, type \TypeName{n} and \TypeName{n}.
\end{WPSteps}
\EndReplay
