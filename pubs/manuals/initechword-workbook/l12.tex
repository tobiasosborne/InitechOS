\Lesson{12}{Moving Sentences and Paragraphs}
\LessonFeatures{Move, Sentence/Paragraph/Page; Move, Block}
To move a whole sentence or paragraph you need not block it: Move can find
its ends for you.  In this lesson you try two changes to the memo -- the
questions paragraph moved first, and a sentence moved to the end of its
paragraph -- without saving them.

\BeginReplay{l12}
\Task{Moving a Paragraph}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \Press{Retrieve}{Shift-F10}, type \TypeName{memo} and press
\PressKey{Enter}.
\item Press \PressKey{Down} twelve times to put the cursor on the first line
of the paragraph that begins \textit{If you have
questions}.\Keys{Down Down Down Down Down Down Down Down Down Down Down}
\item Press \Press{Move}{Ctrl-F4} and select \Choose{Paragraph}{2}.
InitechWord highlights the paragraph, through its hard return, and shows
\Scr{1 Move; 2 Copy; 3 Delete; 4 Append: 0}.
\end{WPSteps}
\CompareScreen[rows=18]{l12-1}
\begin{WPSteps}[resume]
\item Select \Choose{Move}{1}.
\item Press \PressKey{Up}\Keys{Up Up Up} four times to go to the start of the
first paragraph, and press \PressKey{Enter}.
\end{WPSteps}
\CompareScreen[rows=18]{l12-2}
The questions paragraph now comes first.  It moved without its blank line,
which stayed behind; delete or add blank lines with Backspace and Enter as
you need.

\Task{Moving a Sentence}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \PressKey{Down}\Keys{Down Down} three times to go to the second
line of the paragraph that begins \textit{Beginning Monday}, the line that
begins with \textit{sheet.}
\item Press \PressKey{End}, then \PressKey{Home}, \PressKey{Left}, and
\PressKey{Right} ten times to put the cursor in the sentence \textit{The
change takes effect immediately.}\Keys{Right Right Right Right Right Right Right Right Right}
\item Press \Press{Move}{Ctrl-F4}, select \Choose{Sentence}{1}, then
\Choose{Move}{1}.
\end{WPSteps}
\CompareScreen[rows=18]{l12-3}

\Task{Leaving Without Saving}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \Press{Cancel}{F1}: the sentence stays out of the memo.  You can
still put it back with Move's Retrieve, but you are not saving these changes.
\item Press \Press{Exit}{F7}, type \TypeName{n}, then \TypeName{n}.
\end{WPSteps}
\EndReplay
