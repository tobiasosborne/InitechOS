\Lesson{3}{Memo (Second Draft)}
\LessonFeatures{Retrieve; Cursor Movement; Delete; Save}
Bill has read the first draft and wants two changes: the cover sheets are
on the third floor, not the second, and he wants the memo to say that the
change takes effect immediately.  In this lesson you retrieve the memo,
move the cursor to the places to change, and save the second draft.

\BeginReplay{l03}
\Task{Retrieving the Memo}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \Press{Retrieve}{Shift-F10}.  The status line shows
\Scr{Document to be Retrieved:}.
\PullDown{Select \Mi{R}etrieve from the \Mi{F}ile menu.}
\item Type \TypeName{memo} and press \PressKey{Enter}.
\end{WPSteps}
The memo appears with the cursor at the top, and its name on the status
line.
\CompareScreen[rows=24]{l03-1}

\Task{Moving the Cursor}
The arrow keys move the cursor one character or one line at a time; Home
moves it farther.
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \PressKey{Down} eight times to reach the first line of the
first paragraph.  The blank lines count: each is a line of its
own.\Keys{Down Down Down Down Down Down Down}
\item Press \PressKey{End} to move to the end of that line, and
\PressKey{Home}, \PressKey{Left} to move back to its start.
\item Press \PressKey{Home}, \PressKey{Home}, \PressKey{Down} to go to the end
of the memo, and \PressKey{Home}, \PressKey{Home}, \PressKey{Up} to come back
to the top.
\end{WPSteps}

\Task{Changing the Floor}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \PressKey{Down} nine times to reach the second line of the
first paragraph, the one that ends with \textit{second}.\Keys{Down Down Down Down Down Down Down Down}
\item Press \PressKey{End} to go to the end of the line, then press
\PressKey{Left} six times to put the cursor on the \textit{s} of
\textit{second}.\Keys{Left Left Left Left Left}
\item Press \PressKey{Delete} six times to erase \textit{second}.\Keys{Delete Delete Delete Delete Delete}
\item Type \TypeRun{third}.  The text you type goes in at the cursor, and the
rest of the paragraph moves along to make room.
\end{WPSteps}
\CompareScreen[rows=24]{l03-2}

\Task{Adding a Sentence}
The first sentence of the paragraph ends at the start of this line, with
\textit{sheet.}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \PressKey{Home}, \PressKey{Left} to go to the start of the line,
then \PressKey{Right} six times to put the cursor just after the period.\Keys{Right Right Right Right Right}
\item Type the new sentence, with a space in front of it:
\Type{ The change takes effect immediately.}
\end{WPSteps}
InitechWord wraps the paragraph again as you type, so its lines always end
where they should.
\CompareScreen[rows=24]{l03-3}

\Task{Saving the Second Draft}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \Press{Save}{F10}.  The status line offers the memo's own name,
\Scr{MEMO}.
\item Press \PressKey{Enter} to save under that name.  The status line asks
\Scr{Replace A:\wbs MEMO? No (Yes)}: the first draft is already on the disk
under that name.
\item Type \TypeName{y} to replace the first draft with the second.
\end{WPSteps}

\Task{Clearing the Screen}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \Press{Exit}{F7}, then type \TypeName{n} and \TypeName{n}.
\end{WPSteps}
\EndReplay
In Lesson 4 you make the subject line bold and finish the memo.
