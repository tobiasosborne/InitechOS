\Gates{test-pubs-iword}
\Lesson{4}{Memo (Final Draft)}
\LessonFeatures{Bold; Underline; Cursor Movement; Save; Exit}
Bill's final changes are two closing lines: a warning in bold, and a
pointer to the instructions with the word \textit{attached} underlined.  In
this lesson you add them as you type, with Bold and Underline, and save the
finished memo.

\BeginReplay{l04}
\Task{Retrieving the Memo}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \Press{Retrieve}{Shift-F10}, type \TypeName{memo} and press
\PressKey{Enter}.
\item Press \PressKey{Home}, \PressKey{Home}, \PressKey{Down} to move to the
end of the memo, after \textit{collating.}
\end{WPSteps}

\Task{Typing a Bold Line}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \PressKey{Enter} twice to start a new paragraph after a blank
line.\Keys{Enter}
\item Press \Press{Bold}{F6}.  Nothing seems to happen, but InitechWord has
put the start and end of bold in the memo, with the cursor between them.
\item Type the warning:
\Type{Reports without a cover sheet will be returned.}
\item Press \Press{Bold}{F6} again.  The cursor moves past the end of the
bold.
\end{WPSteps}
\CompareScreen[rows=16]{l04-1}
The warning is shown in bold on the screen.

\Task{Typing an Underlined Word}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \PressKey{Enter} twice.\Keys{Enter}
\item Type \TypeRun{Please read the } (with the space at the end).
\item Press \Press{Underline}{F8}, type \TypeRun{attached} and press
\Press{Underline}{F8} again.
\item Type \TypeRun{ instructions before Monday.} (with the space at the
start).
\end{WPSteps}
\CompareScreen[rows=18]{l04-2}

\Task{Saving the Final Draft}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \Press{Save}{F10} and \PressKey{Enter}.
\item At \Scr{Replace A:\wbs MEMO? No (Yes)} type \TypeName{y}.
\end{WPSteps}

\Task{Clearing the Screen}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \Press{Exit}{F7}.  The memo is saved, so type \TypeName{n} at
\Scr{Save Document?}, then \TypeName{n} at \Scr{Exit WP?}.
\end{WPSteps}
\EndReplay
The memo is finished.  Lesson 6 looks at it again, to see the codes that
Bold and Underline put in it.  Lesson 5 first shows you how to move around
a longer document.
