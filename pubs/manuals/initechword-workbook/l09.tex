\Lesson{9}{Blocking Text}
\LessonFeatures{Block; Block, Attributes; Bold; Underline; Cancel}
Bold and Underline can be pressed before you type, as in Lesson 4.  For text
that is already typed, you block it first: mark it with Block, then press
the key.  In this lesson you make the title of the procedure bold and
underline two words of it.

\BeginReplay{l09}
\Task{Blocking the Title}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \Press{Retrieve}{Shift-F10}, type \TypeName{procs} and press
\PressKey{Enter}.  The cursor is at the start of the title.
\item Press \Press{Block}{Alt-F4}.  \Scr{Block on} appears at the left of the
status line.
\item Press \PressKey{End}.  The block stretches to the end of the title.
\end{WPSteps}
\CompareScreen[rows=9]{l09-1}
\begin{WPSteps}[resume]
\item Press \Press{Bold}{F6}.  The title is bold, and Block is off.
\end{WPSteps}

\Task{Blocking Backward}
A block can be stretched backward as well as forward.
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \PressKey{Down} four times to go to the line
\textit{clearly, using a black pen.}, then \PressKey{End}.\Keys{Down Down Down}
\item Press \PressKey{Left} once to put the cursor on the period.
\item Press \Press{Block}{Alt-F4}, then press \PressKey{Left} nine times: the
block runs back over \textit{black pen}.\Keys{Left Left Left Left Left Left Left Left}
\item Press \Press{Underline}{F8}.
\end{WPSteps}
\CompareScreen[rows=9]{l09-2}

\Task{Turning Block Off}
If you turn Block on by mistake, Cancel turns it off without changing
anything.
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \Press{Block}{Alt-F4} and \PressKey{Right} twice.\Keys{Right}
\item Press \Press{Cancel}{F1}.  The highlight and \Scr{Block on} go.
\end{WPSteps}

\Task{Saving the Procedure}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \Press{Save}{F10} and \PressKey{Enter}, and type \TypeName{y} to
replace the earlier version.
\item Press \Press{Exit}{F7}, then type \TypeName{n} and \TypeName{n}.
\end{WPSteps}
\EndReplay
