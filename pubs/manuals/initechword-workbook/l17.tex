\Lesson{17}{Margins}
\LessonFeatures{Margins, Left and Right; Format; Reveal Codes; Save}
A passage quoted in a letter stands out when its margins are narrower than
the rest.  In this lesson you write a short letter to Rosa Martinez, setting
one and a half inch margins for the quoted paragraph and returning to one
inch margins after it.

\BeginReplay{l17}
\Task{Typing the Opening}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Type \TypeRun{Dear Ms. Martinez:} and press \PressKey{Enter}
twice.\Keys{Enter}
\item Type
\Type{As you asked, here is the part of the new procedure that applies to your department:}
\item Press \PressKey{Enter} twice.\Keys{Enter}
\end{WPSteps}

\Task{Narrowing the Margins}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \Press{Format}{Shift-F8} and select \Choose{Line}{1}.  The
Format: Line screen shows the margins in effect: 1 inch each.
\item Select \Choose{Margins}{7}.  The cursor moves to the left margin.
\item Type \TypeName{1.5} and press \PressKey{Enter}.  The cursor moves to the
right margin.
\item Type \TypeName{1.5} and press \PressKey{Enter}.
\end{WPSteps}
\CompareScreen[rows=21]{l17-1}
\begin{WPSteps}[resume]
\item Press \Press{Exit}{F7} to return to the document.
\item Type the quoted paragraph:
\Type{Every TPS report must carry the new cover sheet. Reports without one will be returned to the sender for correction.}
\item Press \PressKey{Enter} twice.\Keys{Enter}
\end{WPSteps}

\Task{Returning to the Standard Margins}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \Press{Format}{Shift-F8}, select \Choose{Line}{1} and
\Choose{Margins}{7}, type \TypeName{1}, press \PressKey{Enter}, type
\TypeName{1} and press \PressKey{Enter}.
\item Press \Press{Exit}{F7}.
\item Type \TypeRun{Please call me if you have any questions.}
\end{WPSteps}
\CompareScreen[rows=10]{l17-2}
The quoted paragraph is indented half an inch from each side.  Its lines are
shorter, so it wraps sooner.

\Task{Checking the Codes}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \PressKey{Up} three times to put the cursor on the quoted
paragraph, and press \Press{Reveal Codes}{Alt-F3}.\Keys{Up Up}
\end{WPSteps}
\CompareScreen[rows=20]{l17-3}
The margin codes stand where you set them, one before the paragraph and
one after it:
\par\noindent\hspace*{4mm}\Code{[L/R Mar:1.5",1.5"]}\quad\Code{[L/R Mar:1",1"]}\par\noindent  The ruler shows
the margins in effect at the cursor; a margin that does not fall on a tab
stop is shown by a bracket instead of a brace.

\Task{Saving the Letter}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \Press{Reveal Codes}{Alt-F3}.
\item Press \Press{Save}{F10}, type \TypeName{martinez} and press
\PressKey{Enter}.
\item Press \Press{Exit}{F7}, type \TypeName{n} and \TypeName{n}.
\end{WPSteps}
\EndReplay

margin.
