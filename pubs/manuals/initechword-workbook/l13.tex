\Lesson{13}{Deleting Text}
\LessonFeatures{Block, Delete; Move, Sentence/Paragraph/Page; Page Break, Hard; Delete}
Backspace and Delete erase a character at a time.  For more, block the text
and delete the block, or let Move find a sentence, a paragraph or a page and
delete it.  In this lesson you take the report of Lesson 8 apart, without
saving the result.

\BeginReplay{l13}
\Task{Deleting a Block}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \Press{Retrieve}{Shift-F10}, type \TypeName{report} and press
\PressKey{Enter}.
\item Press \PressKey{Down} twice to go to the line \textit{Report
Number}.\Keys{Down}
\item Press \Press{Block}{Alt-F4} and \PressKey{Down} to block the line.
\item Press \PressKey{Delete}.
\end{WPSteps}
\CompareScreen[rows=7]{l13-1}
\begin{WPSteps}[resume]
\item Type \TypeName{y}.  The line is deleted.
\end{WPSteps}

\Task{Deleting a Sentence}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \PressKey{Home}, \PressKey{Home}, \PressKey{Down} to go to the end
of the report, on its second page.
\item Press \Press{Move}{Ctrl-F4} and select \Choose{Sentence}{1}.  The
sentence is highlighted.
\item Select \Choose{Delete}{3}.  Move deletes it without asking.
\end{WPSteps}
\CompareScreen[rows=6]{l13-2}

\Task{Deleting the Hard Page}
The second page is empty now.  Its hard page is a code at the end of the
date line, and the cursor is just after it.
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \PressKey{Backspace}.  The hard page is deleted -- InitechWord
does not ask about hard pages -- and the bar goes.
\end{WPSteps}
\CompareScreen[rows=4]{l13-3}

\Task{Clearing the Screen}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \Press{Exit}{F7}, type \TypeName{n} and \TypeName{n}.  The report
on the disk is as Lesson 8 saved it.
\end{WPSteps}
\EndReplay
In Lesson 14 you give text other appearances: italic, double underline and
small capitals.
