\Lesson{22}{Special Techniques}
\LessonFeatures{Caps Lock; Pull-Down Menus; Cancel; Cursor Movement; Codes; Exit}
This lesson is a set of short exercises, each with a side head of its own.
Try them in any order; each starts and ends with a clear screen.

\BeginReplay{l22}
\Task{Watching Caps Lock}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press the Caps Lock key and type \TypeRun{AGENDA}.\CapsLock{on}\Keys{End}
\item Look at the status line: \Scr{Pos} is shown in capitals, \Scr{POS},
while Caps Lock is on.
\end{WPSteps}
\CompareScreen[rows=1]{l22-1}
\begin{WPSteps}[resume]
\item Press Caps Lock again to turn it off.\CapsLock{off}
\item Press \Press{Exit}{F7}, type \TypeName{n} and \TypeName{n}.
\end{WPSteps}

\Task{Using the File Menu}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Type \TypeRun{Menu practice.}
\item Point to \textbf{File} in the menu bar, hold down the mouse button,
drag to \textbf{Save} and release it.\PullChoose{File}{Save}  The status line
shows \Scr{Document to be Saved:}, as if you had pressed F10.
\item Press \Press{Cancel}{F1}.
\item Press \Press{Exit}{F7}, type \TypeName{n} and \TypeName{n}.
\end{WPSteps}

\Task{Escaping From a Menu}
\K{Esc} backs out of menus and prompts as Cancel does.
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \Press{Font}{Ctrl-F8}.  The Font menu appears.
\item Press \PressKey{Esc}.  The menu goes.
\end{WPSteps}

\Task{Getting Before the Codes}
Home, Left goes to the first character of a line, after any codes there.
To put the cursor in front of the codes as well, use three Homes.
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \Press{Bold}{F6}, type \TypeRun{Bold line}, and press
\Press{Bold}{F6}.
\item Press \PressKey{Home}, \PressKey{Left}, then
\Press{Reveal Codes}{Alt-F3}: the highlight is on the \textit{B}, after
\Code{[BOLD]}.
\item Press \PressKey{Home}, \PressKey{Home}, \PressKey{Home},
\PressKey{Left}: the highlight is on \Code{[BOLD]}.
\end{WPSteps}
\CompareScreen[rows=13]{l22-2}
\begin{WPSteps}[resume]
\item Press \Press{Reveal Codes}{Alt-F3}.
\item Press \Press{Exit}{F7}, type \TypeName{n} and \TypeName{n}.
\end{WPSteps}

\Task{Holding Shift Without Meaning To}
If you hold Shift, Ctrl or Alt by mistake while you press Backspace, Delete,
Enter, Tab or a cursor key, nothing goes wrong: InitechWord does exactly
what the key alone does.
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Type \TypeRun{Typo} and press \PressKey{Shift-Backspace}.  The
\textit{o} is erased, as with Backspace.
\item Press \Press{Exit}{F7}, type \TypeName{n} and \TypeName{n}.
\end{WPSteps}
\EndReplay
