\Lesson{26}{Fill-In Form}
\LessonFeatures{Tab; Underline; Reveal Codes; Save}
A form needs lines for people to write on.  Underline draws a line under
everything between its codes, spaces included, so a run of underlined
spaces makes a writing line.  In this lesson you make a cover sheet form
whose lines all begin at the same tab stop.

\newcommand\FormLine[3]{\Typing{#1}\Keys{#2}\Keys{F8}\TypeSpaces{30}\Keys{F8 Enter Enter}%
  \noindent\hspace*{4mm}\makebox[38mm][l]{\textbf{#1}}\textit{#3}, then F8, 30 spaces, F8, Enter twice\par}
\BeginReplay{l26}
\Task{Typing the Title}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Type \TypeRun{TPS REPORT COVER SHEET} and press \PressKey{Enter}
twice.\Keys{Enter}
\end{WPSteps}

\Task{Typing the Fields}
For each field type its name, press Tab until the cursor is at the one and a
half inch stop, press \FK{Underline}{F8}, press the space bar thirty times,
press \FK{Underline}{F8} again, and press Enter twice.
\par\addvspace{4pt}
\begingroup\setlength\parskip{1pt}
\FormLine{Report number:}{Tab}{one tab}
\FormLine{Prepared by:}{Tab}{one tab}
\FormLine{Department:}{Tab}{one tab}
\FormLine{Date:}{Tab Tab}{two tabs}
\endgroup
\par\addvspace{4pt}
\CompareScreen[rows=11]{l26-1}
The spaces between the underline codes are drawn underlined, so each field
has its line.

\Task{Checking the Codes}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \PressKey{Home}, \PressKey{Home}, \PressKey{Up}, then
\Press{Reveal Codes}{Alt-F3}.
\end{WPSteps}
\CompareScreen[rows=22]{l26-2}

\Task{Saving the Form}
\begin{WPSteps}
\item Press \Press{Reveal Codes}{Alt-F3}.
\item Press \Press{Save}{F10}, type \TypeName{form} and press \PressKey{Enter}.
\item Press \Press{Exit}{F7}, type \TypeName{n} and \TypeName{n}.
\end{WPSteps}
\EndReplay
