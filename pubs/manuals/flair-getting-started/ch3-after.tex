\chapter{After Starting FLAIR}

This chapter takes you through a first session with FLAIR: looking at the
data disk, opening an application, typing a few lines, and shutting the
computer down.  It takes about ten minutes.  The \doctitle{FLAIR User's
Guide} teaches each of these skills in full.

\section{Looking at the data disk}
\Operation{FINDER-OPEN-VOLUME}
\begin{TaskSteps}
\Step Move the mouse until the pointer is on the \texttt{INITECH} icon, at
the top right of the screen.
\Step Press and release the mouse button twice, quickly, without moving
the mouse.
\Result{This is called double-clicking.  A window titled \texttt{Drive A
Files} opens.  It shows the top level of the data disk: the file
\file{README.TXT} and the folder \file{APPS}.}
\end{TaskSteps}

\FlairScreen[label=fig:gs-rootwin]{g_rootwin}{The Drive A Files window}

\section{Opening the APPS folder}
\Operation{FINDER-OPEN-FOLDER}
\begin{TaskSteps}
\Step Double-click the \file{APPS} folder.
\Result{The \texttt{APPS} window opens in front.}
\Step Click the zoom box, the first of the two small boxes at the right end
of the window's title bar.
\Result{The window fills the screen and shows all twenty files of the
folder.  The diamonds are applications; the pages are documents and
programs for the InitechDOS prompt.}
\end{TaskSteps}

\FlairScreen[label=fig:gs-apps]{g_apps}{The APPS folder, zoomed}

\section{Writing a memo}
\Operation{TENANT-LOAD}
\begin{TaskSteps}
\Step Double-click \file{TEXT.EXE}.
\Result{InitechText, the text editor, opens with an empty document.}
\Step Type a few lines.  Press \key{Enter} at the end of each paragraph.
\end{TaskSteps}

\FlairScreen[label=fig:gs-text]{g_text}{A memo in InitechText}

\begin{TaskSteps}[resume]
\Step Hold down \key{Ctrl} and press \key{Q}.
\Result{InitechText asks whether to save your changes.  For this practice,
click \texttt{Don't Save}.  InitechText quits.  To keep a document, click
\texttt{Save} and give it a name; the \doctitle{FLAIR User's Guide},
Chapter~5, shows you how.}
\end{TaskSteps}

\section{Shutting down}
\Operation{FLAIR-SHUTDOWN-SAFE}
\begin{TaskSteps}
\Step Click a bare part of the desktop.
\Result{The lower menu bar shows the menus of the Finder.}
\Step Point at \menuname{Special} in the lower menu bar, hold down the
mouse button, move down to \menuitem{Shut Down}, and release the button.
\Result{FLAIR saves the arrangement of your desktop.  The screen goes black
and a message tells you that it is safe to switch off the computer.}
\end{TaskSteps}

\FlairScreen[label=fig:gs-shutdown]{g_shutdown}{It is safe to switch off}

\begin{TaskSteps}[resume]
\Step Turn off the computer, then the display.
\end{TaskSteps}
Always shut down this way before you turn off the computer.
