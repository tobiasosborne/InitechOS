\chapter{Learning About FLAIR}
\index{books}

The FLAIR books are written to be read in this order.

\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{44mm}p{20mm}p{30mm}@{}}
\textbf{Book} & \textbf{Part} & \textbf{Read it to}\\\hline
\doctitle{Getting Started with FLAIR} & 1031-0000-11 & set up and start
FLAIR\\
\doctitle{FLAIR User's Guide} & 1031-0000-10 & learn FLAIR (Part I) and
look things up (Part II)\\
\doctitle{FLAIR Accessories Guide} & 1031-0000-12 & use the Calculator,
InitechText, InitechCalc and InitechPaint\\
\doctitle{FLAIR Quick Reference} & 1031-0000-20 & find a menu command or a
key at a glance\\
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

For the InitechDOS command prompt, read the \doctitle{InitechDOS User's
Guide} (1024-3300-11) and the \doctitle{InitechDOS Reference}
(1024-3300-10).  Initech 123, InitechWord, InitechBase and the TPS Report
Generator each have books of their own.

\section{Getting help}
\index{Help Desk}
If you cannot find the answer in the books, call the Initech Help Desk at
extension 2504.  Have ready the message on the screen, if there is one,
and the first line of \menuitem{About This Computer...} in the Apple menu,
which names the version of InitechDOS.
