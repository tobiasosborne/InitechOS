\GlossaryTerm{application}A program you use to do your work, such as
InitechText.  An application on the data disk has a name ending in
\file{.EXE}.
\GlossaryTerm{data disk}The disk, labelled INITECH, that holds the
applications and your documents.  FLAIR shows it as the \texttt{INITECH}
icon; InitechDOS calls it drive A.
\GlossaryTerm{desktop}The working area that fills the screen, on which the
icons and windows lie.
\GlossaryTerm{double-click}To press and release the mouse button twice,
quickly, without moving the mouse.
\GlossaryTerm{Finder}The part of FLAIR that shows the disk, folders and
files and lets you organize them.
\GlossaryTerm{folder}A container on the disk for files and other folders.
\GlossaryTerm{icon}A small picture that stands for a disk, a folder, a file
or the Trash.
\GlossaryTerm{menu bar}One of the two bars across the top of the screen
that hold the menu titles.
\GlossaryTerm{pointer}The arrow on the screen that moves when you move the
mouse.
\GlossaryTerm{startup disk}The disk from which the computer loads
InitechDOS and FLAIR.
\GlossaryTerm{Trash}The icon at the bottom right of the desktop, where you
put files and folders to throw them away.
\GlossaryTerm{window}A framed area on the screen that shows the contents of
a disk, a folder or a document.
