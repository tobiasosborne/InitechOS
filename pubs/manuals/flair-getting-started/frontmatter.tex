\FrontChapter{How to Use This Book}
\index{book, how to use}
This book tells you how to set up your computer for FLAIR\tm{}, the
graphical desktop of InitechOS\tm{}, how to start it for the first time, and
what to do if it does not start.

Read Chapters 1 and 2 before you turn on the computer.  Chapter~3 takes you
through your first few minutes with FLAIR.  Chapter~4 tells you which book
to read next.  If FLAIR does not start as described, turn to Chapter~5.
Chapter~6 gives a few habits that keep your work safe, and Appendix~A
lists what is on the two InitechOS disks.

\section{Conventions Used in This Book}
\SideHead{Steps}Tasks are written as numbered steps.  The step itself is in
bold type; the plain text under it tells you what happens.

\SideHead{Menu commands}Commands and menus are in bold type: ``Choose
\menuitem{Shut Down} from the \menuname{Special} menu.''

\SideHead{Keys}Key names are shown like this: \key{Ctrl}, \key{Enter}.

\SideHead{Screens}The figures are reproductions of the screen exactly as
it appeared when the task was carried out, with the clock set to 9:30 in
the morning.
