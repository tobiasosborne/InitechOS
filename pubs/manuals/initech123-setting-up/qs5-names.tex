\chapter{Names, Protection and Recalculation}

This chapter names a tax rate, lets the worksheet wait before it
recalculates, and protects the formulas.  Start with an empty worksheet.

\section{What This Chapter Shows You}
\begin{itemize}
\item Naming a cell and using the name in a formula
\item Recalculating by hand
\item Protecting the formulas
\end{itemize}

\section{Naming a Cell}
\ix{range names}
\begin{Keystrokes}
\Do{Type}{\type{Price}}{in A1, press \kname{RIGHT}, type \type{250} and
  press \kname{DOWN} and \kname{LEFT}}
\Do{Type}{\type{Rate}}{in A2, press \kname{RIGHT}, type \type{.07} and
  press \kname{ENTER}}
\Do{Press}{\kname{SLASH}}{and type \type{r}, \type{n}, \type{c}}
\Do{Type}{\type{rate}}{the name, and press \kname{ENTER}}
\Do{Press}{\kname{ENTER}}{to name the current cell, B2}
\Do{Press}{\kname{DOWN}}{and \kname{LEFT}; type \type{Tax}, press
  \kname{RIGHT}, type \type{+b1*rate} and press \kname{DOWN} and
  \kname{LEFT}}
\Do{Type}{\type{Total}}{in A4, press \kname{RIGHT}, type \type{+b1+b3} and
  press \kname{ENTER}}
\end{Keystrokes}

\section{Recalculating by Hand}
\ix{recalculation}
\begin{Keystrokes}
\Do{Press}{\kname{SLASH}}{and type \type{w}, \type{g}, \type{r}, \type{m}}
\Do{Press}{\fkey{Goto}{F5}}{type \type{b1} and press \kname{ENTER}}
\Do{Type}{\type{300}}{and press \kname{ENTER}}
\end{Keystrokes}
B1 changes, but the tax and the total wait, and the \ind{CALC} indicator
appears.  Press \fkey{Calc}{F9} to bring them up to date.
\FlairShot[fig:qs5]{qs5}{Manual recalculation: the tax and total wait for CALC}

\section{Protecting the Formulas}
\ix{protection}
Unprotect the cells you will change, then turn protection on:
\begin{Keystrokes}
\Do{Press}{\kname{SLASH}}{type \type{r}, \type{u}, type \type{b1..b2} and
  press \kname{ENTER}}
\Do{Press}{\kname{SLASH}}{and type \type{w}, \type{g}, \type{p}, \type{e}}
\end{Keystrokes}
Typing in B3 or B4 now displays \Msg{Cell is protected}.  Return to
automatic recalculation with \slashcmd{/Worksheet Global Recalculation
Automatic}.

\section{Task Summary}
\begin{LotTable}{p{58mm}p{70mm}}
\LotHead{\textbf{To} & \textbf{You used}}
Name a cell & /Range Name Create, the name and the range\\
Use the name in a formula & Type the name, as in \texttt{+b1*rate}\\
Wait before recalculating & /Worksheet Global Recalculation Manual\\

Allow changes in a range & /Range Unprot\\
Protect everything else & /Worksheet Global Protection Enable\LotEnd
\end{LotTable}
