\chapter{Changing the Look of a Worksheet}

This chapter formats the sales table of Chapter~1.

\section{What This Chapter Shows You}
\begin{itemize}
\item Displaying numbers as dollars
\item Aligning labels
\item Widening a column
\item Showing percentages
\end{itemize}

\section{Formatting and Aligning}
\ix{formats}
\begin{Keystrokes}
\Do{Press}{\kname{SLASH}}{type \type{r}, \type{f}, \type{c} (Range Format
  Currency)}
\Do{Type}{\type{0}}{no decimal places, and press \kname{ENTER}}
\Do{Type}{\type{b2..e6}}{and press \kname{ENTER}}
\Do{Press}{\kname{SLASH}}{type \type{r}, \type{l}, \type{r} (Range Label
  Right)}
\Do{Type}{\type{b1..e1}}{and press \kname{ENTER}}
\Do{Press}{\fkey{Goto}{F5}}{type \type{a1} and press \kname{ENTER}}
\Do{Press}{\kname{SLASH}}{type \type{w}, \type{c}, \type{s} (Worksheet
  Column Set-Width)}
\Do{Type}{\type{10}}{and press \kname{ENTER}}
\end{Keystrokes}
\FlairShot[fig:qs2]{qs2}{The sales table formatted}
The headings were already right-aligned; /Range Label sets the alignment
of every label in a range at once, as you would after typing labels without
a prefix.

\section{Showing Each Region's Share}
\ix{percentages}
Each region's share of the quarter is its total divided by the grand total
in E6.  The reference to E6 is absolute, \texttt{\$E\$6}, so that every
copy divides by it.
\begin{Keystrokes}
\Do{Press}{\fkey{Goto}{F5}}{type \type{f1} and press \kname{ENTER}}
\Do{Type}{\type{"Share}}{and press \kname{DOWN}}
\Do{Type}{\type{+e2/\$e\$6}}{and press \kname{ENTER}}
\Do{Press}{\kname{SLASH}}{type \type{c}, press \kname{ENTER}, type \type{f3}
  and press \kname{ENTER}}
\Do{Press}{\kname{SLASH}}{type \type{c}, type \type{f2..f3} and press
  \kname{ENTER}, type \type{f4} and press \kname{ENTER}}
\Do{Press}{\kname{SLASH}}{type \type{r}, \type{f}, \type{p}
  (Range Format Percent)}
\Do{Type}{\type{1}}{one decimal place, and press \kname{ENTER}}
\Do{Type}{\type{f2..f5}}{and press \kname{ENTER}}
\end{Keystrokes}
\FlairShot[fig:qs2b]{qs2b}{Each region's share of the quarter}
The four shares add up to the whole quarter.
