\chapter{Depreciation Schedule}

The accounting office compares three methods of depreciating an asset
before it chooses one for the books.  This worksheet computes the first
three years of depreciation by the straight-line, sum-of-the-years'-digits
and double-declining-balance methods.

\section{What You Enter}
\begin{itemize}
\item The cost of the asset
\item Its salvage value at the end of its life
\item Its life in years
\end{itemize}

\section{Entering the Data}
\ix{depreciation}
Type \type{Cost}, \type{Salvage} and \type{Life} in A3..A5 and the amounts
in B3..B5.  Type the headings \type{"Year}, \type{"SLN}, \type{"SYD} and
\type{"DDB} in A7..D7 and the years 1 to 3 in A8..A10.  In row 8 enter
\type{@sln(\$b\$3,\$b\$4,\$b\$5)}, \type{@syd(\$b\$3,\$b\$4,\$b\$5,a8)} and
\type{@ddb(\$b\$3,\$b\$4,\$b\$5,a8)}, and copy B8..D8 to rows 9 and 10.
\FlairShot[fig:sa3]{sa3}{Three depreciation methods}

\begin{LotTable}{p{30mm}p{24mm}p{74mm}}
\LotHead{\textbf{Item} & \textbf{Address} & \textbf{Meaning}}
Cost & B3 & What the asset cost\\
Salvage & B4 & Its value at the end of its life\\
Life & B5 & Its life in years\\
SLN & B8..B10 & Straight-line depreciation, the same each year\\
SYD & C8..C10 & Sum-of-the-years'-digits depreciation for the year in
  column A\\
DDB & D8..D10 & Double-declining-balance depreciation for the year in
  column A\LotEnd
\end{LotTable}

\section{Interpreting the Results}
\LotExample{examples/deprec}
The two accelerated methods charge more in the early years than the
straight-line method, and less in the later years.
